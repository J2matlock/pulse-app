# Persona study: Mark Tsosie

## 1. Who I am
Mark Tsosie, 72, retired tool-and-die machinist. I run the St. Anne's volunteer program that my late wife Ruth ran: 50 volunteers, background checks every 2 years for the diocese, driver's license checks every year for the youth drivers, and a count of shifts so I can thank people at the Christmas dinner. I have a tremor in my right hand, so I do everything I can from the keys. Sunday evening, church office, 1366×768, bad coffee.

I used the keyboard for the whole session. I never had to pick up the mouse, and that alone is worth something. Screenshots are in `ux-review-shots/persona-mark/`.

## 2. Diary

**7:05 pm. The password.** The window opens with the cursor already in the password box, and a thick blue ring tells me where I am (`01-first-screen.png`). Good. I type Ruth's name and the year, Tab, and land on a little eye button I didn't ask for, then Tab again to Confirm. The checkbox for the terms gets a ring and takes Space (`05-checkbox-focus.png`). Three more Tabs to "Create password & continue", Enter. About 3 minutes.

**7:08.** "Set up a recovery key?" (`08-after-create.png`). The ring is gone and I have to press Tab once to find myself. I take the key. It's big and plain, 28 letters (`12-recovery-key.png`), and I copy it into Ruth's binder by hand, twice, measuring twice. The "Done" button only counts once I tick "I have saved this". That's how a careful shop runs. 4 minutes.

**7:13. Welcome.** "Pulse keeps track of the people and things you're responsible for." People and things, not clients. I put in my name, "Volunteer Coordinator", "St. Anne's Parish" (`22-back-focus.png`). The gray example words in the empty boxes ("Coordinator", "Community Services") look as if somebody already filled them in, and that gave me a turn, but they go away when I type.

**7:15. How do you want to start?** Three big boxes with a clear ring on each (`24-tab.png`). "Start from an example." A list opens full of caseload this and social-worker that, and my heart sinks a little (`27-examples.png`). Then I see a search box with the cursor already in it. I type "volunteer" and up come **"Youth volunteer safety: for churches…"** and **"Volunteer manager: …hours given and thank-yous"** (`30-search-volunteer.png`, `32-tab.png`). Somebody thought of us. I sit back and drink some of the bad coffee.

On the way I pressed Tab too many times, and the ring left the box and went behind the gray curtain where I couldn't see it (`29-focus-escaped-dialog.png`). Two more Tabs brought it back.

**7:18.** I pick Volunteer manager. It tells me plainly what I'm getting: "Volunteers: People · 13 columns · deadline rules: Orientation, Background check…" I open "Show its columns" with Enter, read the list, and switch off "Add sample data" with Space (`40-sample-unchecked.png`). I won't have made-up people mixed in with my real ones. "Add", then "Open my list".

**7:20. My list.** "Volunteers. Add Volunteer. No volunteers yet. Add your first volunteer (or press Ctrl+N)" (`44-first-look.png`). Not one word about clients. Ruth would have nodded at this. The best moment of the night so far.

**7:22. The first volunteer.** Ctrl+N puts me straight into the name box (`48-ctrl-n.png`). A little bubble pops up about an icon, I get flustered and wander off, and later find an empty volunteer with no name sitting in my list. Second try: "Rosa Delgado", Enter. It saves, and then **my place disappears**. No cell is lit, and the arrow keys do nothing (`51-right.png`). I press Tab and the ring goes up to the top-left corner of the screen. I'm back at the front door.

**7:25. The long walk.** I count Tabs the way I used to count thread turns. Skip link, sidebar, search box, undo buttons, the toolbar, then three stops in every column header (the name, a tiny clock, a tiny "⋯") for 14 columns. **66 Tabs** before I reach a cell (`57-tab-60.png`, `58-tab-70.png`). Along the way, the ring on the sidebar items is only a thin blue line above and below the word, not a box (`53-sidebar-focus-tasks.png`). Once I counted one too many, pressed Enter and **locked the program** (`61-oops-locked.png`). It asked for my password with the hint showing, and I got back in. No harm done, but my coffee went cold.

**7:32. Help.** Help has a search box. "keyboard" brings up one answer (`65-help-search-keyboard.png`). I press Tab once to reach it and land on "Take the tour" instead, which starts a 26-step tour (`68-tour-by-accident.png`). Escape gets me out and puts me back in the search box, which was kind. It took 15 Tabs, past every category button, to reach the answer. The answer is good: "Press ? at any time." I press ? and get a proper list: arrows, Tab, Home/End, Enter or F2 to edit, Escape, Shift+F10 for menus, Alt+arrows for the next volunteer (`72-shortcuts-sheet.png`). That's a good tool. I'd tape it to the monitor if it printed.

**7:36.** Fiddling about, I find that **Shift+Tab twice from nowhere** drops me right into the first cell. Nobody told me that. I found it the way you find a stripped bolt, by feel.

**7:38. Dates.** Arrows move me cell to cell with a big blue box, and Enter opens a date box with "mm" lit up. I type 11022024 for Harold and it fills in as 11/02/2024 without me typing the slashes. But Enter to save drops me again. The **box is still drawn around the cell, but the arrows are dead** (`85-after-date-enter.png`, `86-arrow-up.png`). That's worse than no box at all, because it lies to me. The shortcut sheet said Tab moves to the next cell, so I try typing the date and then pressing **Tab** instead. That saves it and keeps my place (`88-after-date-tab.png`). From then on I use Tab, never Enter.

**7:42. Where's the color?** The Background check header says "Every 2 years", the same as the diocese. Rosa's check runs out in 13 days and Harold's in 18. No color anywhere. The dashboard says **"All clear! No deadlines need attention"** (`90-dashboard.png`). If I'd believed it, Rosa would have lapsed.

**7:45. The bad one.** I open the column's "⋯" menu to look at the rule. The menu moves nicely with the arrow keys. I count 14 down to "Deadline rule…". My hand jumps, it's 15, I press Enter, and the **whole Background check column is gone**, my dates with it (`94-deadline-rule-dialog.png`). There was no "are you sure". There's a black box in the corner with "Undo", but my ring isn't on it, and by the time I've read it, it's gone.

The menu said "Move column to the Trash", so I go to Trash. **"The Trash is empty"** (`97-trash.png`). My stomach turns over. Help search for "deleted column": "No questions match." For "remove column" there's an answer: "restore it from List settings for 30 days" (`100-help-remove-column.png`). More → List settings, and there it is, "Background check · 2 values · 30 days left · Restore" (`101-list-settings.png`). The window opens with my ring on Cancel at the bottom. I Tab around to Restore, and once I overshoot onto "Delete forever". Thank the Lord, that one makes me **type the column's name to confirm** (`104-delete-forever-confirm.png`), so I Escape. Restore gives me "'Background check' is back, with its values" (`106-after-restore.png`). About 12 minutes and ten years off my life. (Later I tested Ctrl+Z straight after a deletion, and it brings the column back at once (`160-ctrl-z-same-session.png`). I wish I'd known that at 7:45.)

**7:58. The rule.** This time I get to "Deadline rule…" by pressing End and then one up arrow. The window explains it all in plain words: coming up from 60 days, due soon from 30, urgent from 14, with a little shape beside each color (`107-deadline-rule.png`). But my ring starts on **"Remove from this column"**. One shaky double-press and I'd have lost the rule too.

Nothing in that window tells me why there's no color. I look at my columns and see one called "Needs background check". It's empty. I guess that might be it. It's a Yes/No list, and Enter, Down, Enter sets it to Yes. **Color, at last**: Harold yellow with a square, Rosa orange with a diamond (`122-colors-after-yes.png`). The dashboard now says **"Next: Rosa Delgado, Background check in 13 days"**, with Done / Done on… / Extend… buttons (`123-dashboard-after-yes.png`). I'll call Mrs. Delgado Tuesday, before the diocese calls me. That's what I came for.

Then I tested what I feared. I entered Ernest's check as 01/01/2024, which ran out in January, and left "Needs background check" empty. **No color, and the dashboard says 0 overdue** (`164-overdue-but-no-color.png`). With 50 people, I'll miss one. I know I will.

**8:05.** Moving left along a row toward "Needs background check", the lit cell **slid under the frozen Name column** and I couldn't see it (`108-needs-bg-cell.png`). Two more presses and the screen caught up (`112-left6.png`).

**8:08. The empty volunteer.** Left arrow from the first cell of the empty row jumped up to the **last cell of Rosa's row**, and Shift+F10 opened a menu ending in "Move to the Trash" (`124-shift-f10.png`). The menu doesn't say whose it is. I could only tell from where it sat on the screen. I backed out, went down to the empty row, and that time it worked. "'Unnamed volunteer' moved to the Trash", with an Undo (`126-trash-blank.png`).

**8:12. Driver's licenses.** Add column. "Driver's license check", Type: I type "Da" and it picks Date. Then it asks **"Does this date come due again? No / Yes, every [12] [months]"** (`129-type-date.png`). That's exactly how I'd ask it. Down arrow to Yes, Tab, Tab, Add. Done in 2 minutes. That's how the whole program ought to feel.

**8:15. Adding more people.** Once I'd learned the trick, it goes quickly. Ctrl+N, type the name, Tab, Tab, type the date digits straight onto the cell, Tab (`162-type-to-edit.png`, `163-after-tab.png`). The list re-sorts and my ring goes with the person. I could do 50 this way over a few Sundays, as long as I remember the "Needs background check" Yes for each one.

**8:20. Shifts.** I open Rosa with Shift+F10 → "Open volunteer". Her page shows her deadlines in plain cards (`135-rosa-page.png`). Halfway down the page, in plain text, it says: **"// On the page, not in a card (Q8); the line under the heading row ends the section above"** (`136-leaked-comment.png`). Somebody left their pencil notes on the blueprint.

"+ Note" is 16 Tabs in. The kinds are **Note, Shift, Training, Thank-you, Call**, and there's an "Hours given" box (`137-note-form.png`). Shift and Thank-you. Somebody did think of us. I Shift+Tab back to "Shift", press Enter, Tab to the box, type "Thursday meal, served and stayed to wash up", Tab, 3 hours. It says "Ctrl+Enter saves", but **Ctrl+Enter from the hours box does nothing** (`142-shift-note-saved.png`). I Tab three times to Add instead. Saved, and my place disappears again.

Alt+← takes me straight to Harold's page (`152-alt-left-harold.png`), which is lovely. His shift was last Saturday, so I open "More options (when)". A "When: 10/15/2026" box appears above me. Shift+Tab lands on the **tiny calendar icon** inside the box (`155-when-filled.png`), my typed digits go nowhere, and it saved the shift as today. The second time I worked out to Shift+Tab once more and arrow left past the year, and the date took. It's still this month, so Harold's count is right, but I won't always notice.

**8:35. The count.** Reports → New report. It's a form with numbered steps: 1, which list and what to count (Notes); 2, only some (Type is Shift); Dates (Date, This month); 3, split into lines by (Name). The preview says it in a sentence: "Counting 3 notes in Volunteers where Type is Shift, this month, one line per Name", with a bar for each person (`150-report-grouped.png`). Harold 1, Rosa 2. I name it "Shifts this month (Christmas dinner)" and save it (`151-report-saved.png`). Each new filter row showed up **above** my ring, so I had to Shift+Tab back up to it every time, but it got done. 15 minutes.

**8:50.** I lock up. Two hours, and most of it went to learning how to get around.

## 3. Praises
- **It speaks my language.** "Volunteers", "Add Volunteer", "No volunteers yet", "Next: Rosa Delgado, Background check in 13 days". I never once saw "client" on my own screens. It tells me the program is for me too.
- **The example search found us.** Typing "volunteer" turned up a church example and a volunteer manager with background checks every 2 years already set. It saved me an hour and gave me a list Ruth would recognize.
- **"Does this date come due again? Yes, every 12 months."** Adding the driver's license column took two minutes and no guessing.
- **The ring, where it exists, is big and clear.** In cells, buttons, menus and dialogs I can see a thick blue box from across the room. That's what an old man needs.
- **Menus work with arrow keys, End and Escape**, and Escape always put me back where I'd started.
- **The ? shortcut sheet** is plain and complete. Shift+F10, Alt+arrows, and Tab to move on all work as it says.
- **The dashboard.** Colored stripes with a shape for each level (circle, square, diamond), days to go, and Done / Done on… / Extend… right there.
- **Safety where it counts.** "Delete forever" makes me type the name. Trashing a volunteer gives me an Undo. Ctrl+Z puts a deleted column back.
- **The Shift and Thank-you note kinds, and Hours given.** Somebody thought about volunteers.
- **The report builder** answered "how many shifts did each person do this month" in a sentence I could read aloud.

## 5. Suggestions
- "If a date has a rule on it, color it, or at least tell me why it isn't." → When a deadline rule is waiting on another column (like "Needs background check"), show a gray "Not checked: Needs background check is empty" in the cell, on the record page and on the rule window. Or make the example default that column to Yes.
- "Don't put the trash can one notch from the thing I want." → Put "Move column to the Trash" behind a confirmation (or a separator plus a confirmation), and list deleted columns on the Trash page, or have the Trash page say "Deleted columns are under List settings".
- "When I save something, leave me where I was." → After Enter commits a cell, keep focus on that cell. After Add / Save / Restore, put focus back on the control that opened it, or on the new item.
- "Give me a door straight into the list." → Have the skip link (or a key like F6, or Ctrl+G) land on the grid's current cell, and make the header icons one Tab stop per column (arrow keys inside).
- "Start the dangerous windows on the safe button." → In the Deadline rule window, focus the rule summary or Close, not "Remove from this column".
- "Tell me whose menu it is." → Put the volunteer's name at the top of the row menu ("Rosa Delgado"), and stop Left arrow at the first column instead of wrapping to the row above.
- "Somebody sweep the shop floor." → Remove the "// On the page…" text from the record page.
- "Make date boxes take my typing from either side." → On Shift+Tab into a date box, select the month part, and skip the calendar-icon tab stop (or give it a real label and a bigger ring).
- "Let Ctrl+Enter save from anywhere in the note." → Handle Ctrl+Enter on the whole note form.
- "Give the sidebar a full box like everything else." → Use the same 3px outline on sidebar items as on buttons.

## 6. Overall
Would I still be using it in a week? **Yes, probably.** I'd use the dashboard and the shift report, I'd stick to Tab instead of Enter, and every Sunday I'd check each volunteer's "Needs background check" by hand, because I don't trust it to tell me otherwise. If my nephew could make that hidden Yes go away, I'd stop worrying.
