# Bobby Okonkwo-Reyes, 67 — persona session

## 1. Who I am
Retired Dayton desk sergeant, Army vet (Panama, '89), volunteer veterans' benefits navigator at VFW Post 9311, 35 vets on a yellow legal pad. Macular degeneration in the left eye, light mode only, I print everything. Earl Haskins missed his appeal by eleven days on my watch. Not again.

Session setup: window 1280×720, light mode, Pulse's text size set to Largest by me in the first ten minutes. Screenshots are in `ux-review-shots/persona-bobby/`. PDFs I "printed" are rendered as `p-0x-*.png` in the same folder.

## 2. Diary

**8:40.** Coffee, legal pad, the Dell. The program opens on "Create your password" (`01-first-screen.png`). Under the logo it says "Your data is encrypted with it." Good. The letters are on the small side for me. I lean in. First question before I type anything: does this go on the internet? There's a "Privacy Policy" link by the checkbox. I click it, and my web browser opens a page on some company website (`02-privacy-policy.png`, the program itself doesn't change). So it sends me to the internet to tell me whether it goes on the internet. I close the browser. I'll find out some other way.

**8:43.** Password. It wants 12 characters and suggests a short sentence. Fine: the post and the year. I put a hint in (`03-password-filled.png`). Three clicks, two minutes, because I type slowly.

**8:45.** "Set up a recovery key? … Without one, a forgotten password means permanent data loss." (`04-after-password.png`). That's straight talk, and I like straight talk. I click "Show my recovery key" (`06-recovery-key.png`). The key is big, in block letters, and readable. "Print a recovery card" brings up the Windows print dialog and I print it. The card says "Do NOT store it on the same computer." Copy that. It goes in the post's lockbox, not the desk drawer. I tick "I have saved this" and click Done.

**8:48.** Welcome, then my name, title and organization (`09-get-started.png`). Then "How do you want to start?" (`10-setup-next.png`). I don't have a spreadsheet, I have a legal pad. "Start from an example" it is. There's a search box, so I type "veteran" (`12-search-veteran.png`) and up comes **"Veterans service officer: intent-to-file deadlines one year out, claims, decisions and appeal windows."** Somebody who builds these programs knows what an ITF is. I sit back for a second. "Show its columns" (`14-vso-columns.png`): Name, Claim filed, Appeal filed, Next appointment, Branch, Phone, Claim type, Intent to file date, Decision date, Status. I don't see a spot for "VA asked for evidence, 30 days", but most of it's there. I **uncheck "Add sample data"**: I'm not mixing made-up vets with real ones. Add.

**8:50.** "Check for new versions … sends nothing about you or your data" (`15-after-add.png`). A version number only. I leave it on.

**8:51.** My list, empty (`16-grid-first.png`). It's too small for me. Settings. A yellow tip box pops up right over what I'm trying to read (`17-settings.png`). Got it. **Appearance → Text and button size → Largest**, Stronger contrast on, Light (`18-appearance.png` before, `r-03-appearance-largest.png` after). Now I can read it without my nose on the glass. **8:54.** Settings → About (`r-04-about-largest.png`): **"Your data stays on your computer. Nothing goes to the internet, nothing is reported back, and no account is needed."** That's the sentence I was looking for. It took me about 3 minutes to find, inside my 2-minute limit plus one. **8:56.** "Add Veteran" (`r-10-add-veteran.png`). A box says "Type the veteran's name". I type BRENNAN, WALT the way it'd want it, Walt Brennan, and press Tab like in Excel (`r-11-after-name-tab.png`). "Saved", bottom right. Good. There's a little blue clock next to "Claim filed". I move the mouse over to it, slowly, and the header **shifts under my hand**: a drag handle and arrows appear, the clock slides left, and I click the "…" instead (`r-13a-hover-clock.png`). Now I've got a whole menu of "Sort ascending / Freeze columns up to here / Move column to the Trash" (`r-13-claim-rule-popup.png`). Negative. Escape.

**9:00.** The first tip said the little person icon "opens a veteran's page". I click it (`r-14-walt-page.png`). That's more like a file folder. ## 3. Praises
- **The "Veterans service officer" example.** It knows intent-to-file, decision dates and appeal windows. That's 20 minutes of setup I didn't have to figure out, and it told me somebody thought about people like my vets.
- **The program shows its math:** "Due Nov 3, 2026 (12 months after Nov 3, 2025)". I can check it against the letter in my hand. That one line is the Earl problem, solved.
- **The dashboard's "What's due" list.** Worst first, colored, with shapes, and checklist items mixed in. "Next: Tyrone Pettis, Claim filed in 7 days" at the top is the morning briefing I used to give at roll call.
- **Straight answers about privacy:** About ("Nothing goes to the internet") and Help → Security and privacy summary. No cheerleading, just facts. That's why real names went in.
- **Largest text size** works without breaking the screens I used. I could read everything that wasn't grey.
- **Recovery key with a printed card**, and "Back up now" to USB with a plain "Backup saved." Two of my three worries were handled in about four clicks each.

## 4. Frustrations
1. **"Print this week" printed "Nothing due this week" with a vet 7 days out** (`r-35-print-menu.png`, `p-01-print-this-week.png`). "This week" stops on Saturday. My meeting is Monday; I need the next 30 days. *Severity: gave up (that day).* Cost: the one thing I came to do, a page for the commander, I ended up writing by hand.
2. **None of the printouts match the dashboard list.** The list PDF is in typing order, tiny and grey (`p-02-list-save-as-pdf.png`). The supervisor report doesn't name names (`r-41-supervisor-report.png`). The report I built the next morning drops checklist deadlines (Dolores) and calls a 15-day appeal "On track" (`p-03-report-next-30-days.png`). *Severity: needed a workaround (pen).* Cost: a printout that leaves off a vet is worse than no printout. That's how Earl happened.
3. **Checklist due date silently lost** (`r-28-checklist-details.png` → `r-29-checklist-added.png`). I clicked into the date box, typed the date, and it saved with no date and no warning. *Severity: recovered, only because I looked twice.* Cost: Dolores's 30-day evidence deadline nearly vanished.
4. **Deadlines are labeled with what's *not* done yet:** "Appeal filed · Nov 3", "Claim filed · in 7 days" on the dashboard, the page and the PDFs (`r-21-walt-deadlines-after.png`, `r-34-dashboard-scrolled2.png`). *Severity: recovered.* Cost: I have to translate in my head every time, and tired eyes misread.
5. **Numbers that don't agree:** "Due in the next 30 days: 3" over a list with 4 in 30 days; "Due in the next 7 days: 1" next to "This week (0)" (`r-30-dashboard.png`, `r-33-dashboard-scrolled.png`). "Veteran details (3 filled)" with one field filled (`r-17-walt-details.png`). *Severity: recovered.* Cost: I stop trusting the counts and count myself.
6. **Computer writing on every vet's page:** "// On the page, not in a card (Q8)…" (`r-15-walt-deadlines.png`). *Severity: cosmetic.* Cost: it chips at trust. What else did they leave in?
7. **The clock icon moves when I reach for it** and I open the column menu instead (`r-13a-hover-clock.png`, `r-13-claim-rule-popup.png`). *Severity: recovered.* Cost: a scare. "Move column to the Trash" is one wrong click from where I aimed.
8. **Entering a vet is slow at large text.** In the list I can see 3 of 10 columns, and typing a date into a selected cell showed nothing (`r-24-tyrone-typed-date.png`), so every vet means opening the page and scrolling. *Severity: recovered.* Cost: about 3 minutes a vet, close to 2 hours for my 35.
9. **No place for a "VA asked for evidence, 30 days" date** in the veterans example. I had to use a checklist item, which then didn't show up in the 30-day count or the deadline report. *Severity: recovered.*
10. **Grey text:** the "Add…" placeholders, the empty-list hint and the printed tables are grey on white. Even with Stronger contrast on, I squint at those (`r-18-walt-all-fields.png`). *Severity: cosmetic for me on screen, real on paper.*
11. **"Privacy Policy" opens a website** (`02-privacy-policy.png`). I wanted the answer inside the program, before I trusted it with anything. *Severity: recovered (found About later).*

## 5. Suggestions
- "Give me one button that prints what's due in the next 30 days, worst first, in letters I can read."
  → Dashboard Print & export: add "Print what's due (next 30 days)", the same rows as the What's due list (checklist items included), sorted by days left, at 12 pt or larger in black, with the days-left number in bold.
- "Call a deadline what it is."
  → Show the rule name ("Appeal due", "Intent to file expires") as the label on deadline cards, the dashboard and PDFs, and keep the column name ("Appeal filed") for where you mark it done.
- "If I typed a date and it didn't take, stop me."
  → Checklist dialog: block Add item (or warn) when the Due date field is partly filled or invalid, instead of saving the item with no date.
- "Use the same words on paper as on the screen."
  → Report "State" column uses the screen labels and colors (Urgent / Due soon / Coming up / Overdue), not "On track" for something 15 days out.
- "If the box says 3, the list should show 3."
  → The 30-day and 7-day tiles count the same things the What's due list shows (or say "column deadlines only"); line up "This week" with "next 7 days".
- "Take the programmer's note off the page."
  → Remove the leaked comment text under Deadlines on the veteran page; fix "(N filled)" to count filled fields.
- "Hold still while I aim."
  → Keep the deadline-rule clock in the same place on hover (reserve the space for the drag handle and sort arrows), or give it its own larger hit area.
- "The VA's 30-day evidence letters are half my week."
  → Add an "Evidence due" date column with a 30-day rule to the Veterans service officer example.
- "Let me print the security page for the commander."
  → Add a "Print" button next to "Copy for my supervisor" on the Security and privacy summary.
- "Make the report builder offer 'next 30 days'."
  → Add "Next 7 / 30 / 90 days" to the date-range choices when the field is Due date.

But Monday's sheet for the commander I'll still finish by hand until the printout matches the screen and includes every kind of deadline.

*Study notes (not Bobby):*
- With Pulse's text size at Larger/Largest, Playwright's `page.screenshot` captures only the top-left part of the window, so text looks cut off on the right. That's a harness artifact, not an app bug; this session's later shots use a CDP capture. Shots 19–25 from the first pass were deleted for that reason.
- The renderer crashed (SIGTRAP) often at launch across all parallel persona runs (about 80 crashes in an hour, across every agent's data folder). That looks environmental (load), but it may be worth a look if it shows up outside parallel e2e runs.
- Native `<select>` popups (Branch, Claim type) block screenshots while they're open; options were read from the DOM.
- The "Print a recovery card" print was stubbed (a hidden iframe calls `print()`); the card text was confirmed.
- The session clock was Thursday 2026-10-15 (the brief says Tuesday), and the "this week" printout covered Sun Oct 11–Sat Oct 17.
