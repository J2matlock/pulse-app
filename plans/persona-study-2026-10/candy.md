# Candy Beaumont: "Don't you dare lose my note"

## 1. Who I am
I'm Candy Beaumont, 63, RN for 38 years, now a home-health nurse case manager with 25 patients across two parishes. I chart in my Camry between visits on a little 13" laptop, dark mode, brightness down, because my migraines don't care what time my next visit is.

## 2. Diary (Tuesday, 12:30, Sonic parking lot, cherry limeade, 40 minutes until Mr. Arceneaux's neighbor)

**12:30**: I open this thing and get hit with a white screen. Lord. I squint through the password box (`01-first-open.png`). I like that it says "a short sentence like 'blue tractor sings at noon'". I can remember a sentence. I type mine twice, add a hint, tick the terms box. About a minute.

**12:31**: It asks if I want a recovery key (`03-after-password.png`). Yes, honey, I want every safety net you've got. It shows me a long code and says to write it down (`04-recovery-key.png`). I write it on the back of my mileage envelope. There's a checkbox for "I have saved this" before Done. Good, it doesn't let me skip that by accident.

**12:33**: Name, title, agency, all optional. Fine (`06-name-step.png`). Then "How do you want to start?" I pick "Start from an example" because I'm not building anything from scratch in a parking lot.

**12:34**: Lots of examples. I type "home health" in the search and get one card, "Human services", which says social workers and coordinators (`09-search-home-health.png`). That's not me. I try "nurse" and get *School nurse* (`10-search-nurse.png`). I try "patient", "visit", "hospice". Nothing that says home health. I almost back out. Then I click "Human services" anyway and there it is, eighth on the list: **Home Health Coordinator: visits, recertifications, and plan-of-care signatures** (`11-human-services-layouts.png`). Well, why didn't you say so. That took me about two minutes and five searches.

**12:36**: The confirmation shows the columns: Patient Name, Next Visit, Recertification Due, Plan of Care Signed, Physician, Insurance. That's my world (`13-add-details.png`). But it also says it'll call my people "clients" and my notes "case notes". The column says "Patient", the words say "client". Pick one, sugar. I click Add.

**12:37**: My caseload opens full of sample patients and a little popup about Ruth Jackson's red date (`15-first-look.png`). Colors are bright on a white grid. My eyes hurt. **First thing I do is hunt for dark mode.** Settings → Appearance → Dark. One click, and the whole thing goes a soft navy (`23-dark.png`, `24-words.png`). Oh, thank you. It even stays dark every time I open it.

**12:38**: While I'm in Settings I see "Words: call things what you call them." I change client → **patient** and case note → **visit note** and hit Save words (`25-more-words.png`, `26-words-saved.png`). The preview says "Add a patient · 3 patients due this week". Now it talks like me. Isn't that just the sweetest thing.

**12:39**: Scrolling the same page I find **"Mileage on case notes: record miles on case notes for your monthly mileage claim"** and **"Time on case notes"**, and both are *switched off* (`26-features-mileage.png`). The Home Health example didn't turn them on? Home health is *nothing but* mileage and minutes. If I hadn't scrolled I'd never have known. I tick Time, Mileage and Appointments (`27-features-on.png`).

**12:41**: Back on my list, I click "Remove sample data". It tells me plainly that 8 made-up patients go to the Trash and I can bring them back for 30 days (`31-remove-sample-confirm.png`). I like being told where things go.

**12:42**: Add Patient. The cursor lands right in the name box ("Type the patient's name") (`33-add-patient.png`). I type Euclid Arceneaux, Tab, type 10/20/2026 straight into Next Visit (`35-typed-date.png`), Enter. Then **Ctrl+N** (it told me about Ctrl+N on the empty screen) → Delphine Broussard, Enter, Ctrl+N → Odette Guidry. Three patients in under a minute (`37-three-patients.png`). Euclid's date turns orange because it's 5 days out. I can work with this.

*(Side note: the first time, I clicked Add Patient and then got pulled away, and when I came back there was a blank nameless patient in my list (`33b-reopened.png`). I just typed into it. It counted as "1 patient" though.)*

**12:44**: I click the little person icon to open Mr. Arceneaux's page (`40-profile.png`). There's a big blue **+ Visit note** button. A tip box pops up over my checklist telling me to "pick the kind first". OK. Further down the page, between Deadlines and the Case log, there's a line of what looks like programmer talk: **"// On the page, not in a card (Q8); the line under the heading row ends the section above"** (`41-code-comment-on-screen.png`). Somebody left their homework on my screen. Not a big deal, but it makes me wonder what else isn't finished.

**12:45**: Visit note. I click the 🏠 Visit kind and start typing my assessment: vitals, crackles, the 2+ edema, the daughter… and there are **Minutes** and **Miles** boxes right under the note (`42-visit-note-box.png`, `43-half-note.png`). I put 50 minutes in.

**12:47**: *Knock knock.* It's his daughter at my window with a question about his pills. I walk off mid-sentence. **Pulse locks** (`44-locked-midnote.png`, dark lock screen, my hint showing). Twenty minutes later I come back, type my password and… **my note is right there, mid-sentence, "Daughter reports pt", with 50 in Minutes and Visit still picked** (`45-after-unlock.png`). I said "oh, thank God" out loud in a Sonic parking lot. That is the whole reason I'm giving this thing a chance.

**12:49**: It says "Type / to insert a snippet". I type "/" and… nothing happens (`46-slash-snippet.png`). No list, no "you don't have any yet". Just a slash in my note. I backspace it.

**12:50**: I finish the note (the call to Dr. Landry's office, the Lasix order, the teaching), type 23.4 miles, and press **Ctrl+Enter** like it says. The box clears (`48-note-saved.png`). For a second my heart stops, because nothing at the top says "saved". I scroll down and there it is in the Case log: 🏠 Visit · just now · the whole note · **"50 min (3 units) · 23.4 mi"**. Units! It figured my billing units. OK, I forgive you.

**12:52**: Testing it, because of Mr. Thibodeaux. Delphine's page: I type in **Scratch notes** ("Ask Delphine about the stairs… granddaughter phone # is"), hit Ctrl+L, unlock. Still there (`50-scratch-typing.png`, `51-scratch-after-unlock.png`). Good.

**12:53**: I start adding the granddaughter's number under "Add contact info", typing a label and the phone, then Ctrl+L, unlock. **The phone number form is gone** (`52-contact-info.png` → `53-contact-after-unlock.png`). It's only a phone number, but it's the same promise and it didn't keep it.

**12:55**: Calendar. Dark, clean, a month grid with Euclid's Next Visit on the 20th (`60-calendar.png`). "Add…" gives me four plain choices; I pick **Appointment: with a patient** (`61-add-menu.png`). The form is clear (`62-appointment-dialog.png`): What, With (I type "Euc" and pick him), Date, Time, Where. I fill it all in, including his address on Bayou Rd (`64-appt-filled.png`)…

**12:57**: …and the office calls. Pulse locks. I unlock and **the whole appointment form is gone**. Everything I typed, gone, like it never happened (`66-dialog-after-unlock.png`). Oh, honey, no. The visit note kept its words and this little form couldn't? I sigh and type it all again. Two minutes lost, and a little trust too.

**12:59**: Second try. Repeats → **Weekly**, every 1 weeks, the days Sun–Sat as buttons, "No end date" (`67-weekly-options.png`, `68-weekly-days.png`). None of the days are lit up, so I'm not sure if it knows I mean Tuesday. I hit Schedule anyway and it put **every Tuesday at 10:00** on the calendar, and there's an Undo right on the toast (`69-scheduled.png`). Every Tuesday, done. That's exactly what I wanted.

**13:02**: Delphine is "twice a week for three weeks, then weekly." I make one appointment: Weekly, **Mon + Thu**, "Ends after **6** times" (`70-twice-weekly.png`), then a second one, weekly Thursdays starting Nov 12. Agenda view lists them all in order with names (`72-agenda.png`). The month view cuts everything to "1:30 pm SN visit…" so I can't tell who's who without hovering (`71-calendar-month.png`). I'll use Agenda.

**13:05**: Mileage total. I go to the Dashboard first: patients, deadlines, "Next: Euclid Arceneaux". No miles anywhere (`80-dashboard.png`). Reports: two cards, "Case notes per month" (it still says *case* notes even though I changed the word) and "Overdue deadlines" (`81-reports.png`). No mileage. I click **New report** and it's a whole build-your-own form (`82-new-report.png`). I pick the "Visit notes per month" starter, and under "Show these numbers" there's "Add a value", where Total → **Miles** is in the list (`84-add-value.png`). I change Dates to "This month", name it "My mileage this month" (`85-mileage-report.png`): **Oct 2026 · 1 visit · 50 minutes · 23.4 miles.** Save → Show on dashboard.

**13:09**: Back on the Dashboard it's there, at the very bottom under "Recently archived", with the description still saying "How busy each month was, by the visit notes written" (`90-dashboard-mileage-tile.png`). But it *adds itself up*. That took me about four minutes and a fair bit of squinting at dropdowns. Nobody at my agency would find this on their own. Once it's built, though, it's built.

**13:11**: Snippets. The page says "No snippets yet". That's why the "/" did nothing (`91-snippets.png`). I make **/edu**: "Pt/caregiver educated on s/s to report to RN/MD, med regimen, and fall precautions. Verbalized understanding, return demo adequate." (`93-snippet-filled.png`, `94-snippet-saved.png`). On Odette's note I type "/ed"… and a box pops up but **all I can see is its bottom edge**: "Tab inserts · Up or Down, then Enter, picks another · Esc closes". The actual snippet is hidden above the edge of the note card (`95-snippet-popup.png`, `95b-popup-again.png`). I press Enter like any normal person and get a new line instead. I read the tiny hint, backspace, retype, press **Tab**, and the whole education sentence drops in (`96-snippet-expanded.png`). Oh, I'm going to love this. I just had to fight it the first time.

**13:13**: Another interruption test. Odette's note is half written with 40 min and 17.8 mi when the office asks me about Mr. Arceneaux's weight. I click "Back to Home Health Coordinator", open Euclid, look, then go back to Odette. **Her draft is still there, minutes and miles too** (`102-back-to-odette.png`). But the list gave me no sign Odette had a note waiting (`100-left-midnote.png`). If Kaylee's school had called right then, I'd have forgotten it.

**13:15**: The real fear test. Half a note on Odette, and I click the window's X out of habit (or the battery dies, which on this laptop it does). **No warning. The app just closes.** I open it back up and the note box is empty (`111-after-reopen.png`; same thing in `54-before-battery-dies.png` → `57-visit-note-after-restart.png`). Scratch notes survived a restart; the visit note did not. That is *exactly* how I lost Mr. Thibodeaux's last visit. Lock: safe. Close: gone.

**13:16**: Limeade's empty. 46 minutes in. Three patients, two visit notes, a snippet, repeating visits on the calendar, and a mileage total that updates itself. I'm pulling out.

## 3. Praises
- **Dark mode, one click, remembered.** Settings → Appearance → Dark (`23-dark.png`). Every screen, the lock screen included, is easy on my eyes after that. That's what decides whether I can use it in the afternoon at all.
- **My half-written visit note survived the lock** (`45-after-unlock.png`), text, minutes, kind and all, and also survived me wandering to another patient and back (`102-back-to-odette.png`). That's the one thing I came for.
- **Minutes and Miles right in the note box, with billing units figured for me** ("50 min (3 units) · 23.4 mi", `48-note-saved.png`). No separate mileage log, no separate time sheet.
- **"Every Tuesday" really is that easy.** Weekly, the date picks the day, Schedule, Undo on the toast (`69-scheduled.png`). "Ends after 6 times" handles my twice-a-week-for-three-weeks patients (`70-twice-weekly.png`).
- **Ctrl+N / Tab / Enter to add patients fast** (`37-three-patients.png`), and Ctrl+Enter saves a note. I type fast; it keeps up.
- **I can call them patients.** Changing the words took 30 seconds and the whole app followed (`26-words-saved.png`).
- **Snippets, once found**: /edu with Tab drops in my whole teaching sentence (`96-snippet-expanded.png`).
- **Plain, kind messages**: "8 made-up patients will move to the Trash. You can bring them back from there for 30 days." (`31-remove-sample-confirm.png`).

## 5. Suggestions
- "If I'm halfway through a note and that window closes, or the battery dies, I want it waiting for me when I come back. Keep it like you keep my scratch notes, and if I click the X, *ask me* first."
  → Save the visit-note draft (text, kind, minutes, miles) to the database as you type, restore it on reopen, and show an "You have an unsent note for Odette Guidry" confirm on window close.
- "Whatever you do for my visit note, do for every box: the appointment form, the phone number form."
  → Keep open dialogs and inline forms (appointment, contact info) and their values across lock/unlock instead of closing them.
- "If I pick Home Health, turn on mileage and minutes for me. That's half my job."
  → Have the Home Health Coordinator example enable Time on notes, Mileage on notes and Appointments, and list them on the "Turn on the features it uses" line.
- "Give me a 'Mileage this month' button. Just the number, and let me print it for my reimbursement."
  → Add a ready-made "Miles and minutes this month" starter in New report (and pin it by default when Mileage is on), with a matching description.
- "Show me the snippet I'm about to insert, and let Enter put it in."
  → Position the snippet popup below the caret (or outside the card's clipping), and accept Enter as well as Tab; when there are no snippets, show "No snippets yet. Make one" in the popup.
- "Put a little pencil next to a patient who has a note I haven't finished."
  → Show a draft indicator on the grid row and profile header when a visit-note draft exists.
- "Tell me it saved."
  → After Ctrl+Enter, show a brief "Visit note saved" toast or scroll the new entry into view.
- "Let 'home health' find Home Health."
  → Index the Human services sub-layouts (Home Health Coordinator, etc.) in the example search.
- "Take that computer note off my patient's page."
  → Remove the stray "// On the page, not in a card (Q8)…" text from the profile view.

## 6. Overall
Would I still be using Pulse in a week? **Probably yes, cautiously.** The lock-screen draft saving and the minutes/miles on every note are worth it. But until a closed window stops eating my notes, I'll keep a copy of every note in my phone app first. That's the habit this thing was supposed to break.
