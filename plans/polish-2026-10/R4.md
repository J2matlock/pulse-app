# R4: Dashboard, deadline rules and reports

Scope: DashboardView and views/dashboard/**, AlertRuleEditor and RuleColorKey, deadlineCalc.ts, the shared/main deadline engine (including the done flags from migration 046), and Reports (builder, chart, saved reports, starter-reports.ts).

## Bugs

- **[bug]** `src/shared/deadlineEngine.ts:561` (with `src/renderer/views/dashboard/DeadlineRow.tsx:131`). Extending a quota deadline never wears off.
  - What goes wrong: `extensionAnchor()` ties a quota extension to the rule's own cell. Quota rules sit on a Deadline column with no value of its own (behavior-analyst "Sessions left"), so the anchor saved is `''`. Say someone clicks "Extend…" on "Sessions left", then renews the authorization with a new start and end date. The anchor still matches `''`, so the due date stays stuck on the old extended date until someone finds "Remove extension".
  - A second effect: `applyExtension` works the state out again from the new date, so a used-up quota ("All 12 used") drops from overdue to "on track".
  - Fix: anchor quota extensions on the end-date column's value, or hide "Extend…" for quotas, since Renew is their action.

- **[bug]** `src/renderer/views/DashboardView.tsx:205-215`. The dashboard goes stale after an example or sample data is added or removed while it's open.
  - What goes wrong: the empty dashboard's "See an example with sample data" button opens ApplyStarterDialog, which emits `sample-data:changed` (`ApplyStarterDialog.tsx:127`). The dashboard only listens to `deadline:changed` and `followup:changed`. If the person closes the "Added" dialog, the dashboard keeps showing "Nothing here yet" and tiles at 0 until they navigate away. "Remove sample entries" from the global banner leaves the sample deadlines listed in the same way. This is the same class of bug as the stale list after setup.
  - Fix: call `reload()` on `sample-data:changed`, and emit an event after any starter is applied, with or without samples.

- **[bug]** `src/renderer/views/dashboard/cards/RuleKindCards.tsx:16-23`, `src/renderer/views/dashboard/cards/SavedReportCard.tsx:15-22`. The "Due for service", "Running low" and pinned-report cards load once and never refresh.
  - What goes wrong: marking a meter deadline Done in the Priority list, or undoing it, removes the row from the list. The "Due for service" card still lists the item, and a pinned "Overdue deadlines" report keeps its old rows.
  - Fix: reload these cards on `deadline:changed`, and have the dashboard's `reloadDeadlines` / onUndone path emit that event, or pass a refresh counter in.

- **[bug]** `src/renderer/components/profile/DeadlinePanel.tsx:317` (data from `src/renderer/utils/deadlineCalc.ts:63`). On the record page, quota counts don't update after a note is written.
  - What goes wrong: `activityCount` comes from `gridStore.deadlineContext.quota`, which is only loaded with the grid. Writing a counted "session" note on the record page fires `CASE_LOG_CHANGED`, but nothing calls `refreshDeadlineContext()`. The panel keeps saying "5 of 12 left" (and never flips to "All 12 used") until the list is reloaded. Voiding a note or moving its date has the same problem.
  - Fix: in ProfileView (or DeadlinePanel), subscribe to `IPC.CASE_LOG_CHANGED` for this row and call `useGridStore.getState().refreshDeadlineContext()`.

- **[bug]** `src/renderer/views/DashboardView.tsx:357`. The Overdue tab disagrees with the "Overdue deadlines" tile.
  - What goes wrong: `countDeadlineTiles` counts a used-up quota (`isUsedUp`, daysUntil 0) as overdue, and so do the briefing and the "Overdue deadlines" report. The Overdue tab filters on `d.daysUntil < 0` only. The tile says 3, but the tab shows 2 or "Nothing overdue".
  - Fix: use `d.daysUntil < 0 || isUsedUp(d)` there, as WeekViewTab already does.

- **[bug]** `src/shared/deadlineSummary.ts:12`. Record PDFs and the print view say "OVERDUE by 0 days" for a used-up quota.
  - What goes wrong: a used-up quota has `state: 'overdue'` and `daysUntil: 0` while its end date is still ahead. `export-pdf.ts:325` and `ProfilePrintLayout.tsx:159` print "Next due Dec 31, 2026 · OVERDUE by 0 days · All 12 used".
  - Fix: branch on `isUsedUp(d)` first, and print the label ("All 12 used · ends Dec 31, 2026").

- **[bug]** `src/main/report-builder.ts:103-107`. The "Deadline" field in deadline reports can't find columns whose rule was set in the app.
  - What goes wrong: `isDeadlineColumn` checks `config.alert_rule_name`, but the column menu (`ColumnHeader.tsx:124`) and Add Column (`GridToolbar.tsx:235`) store only `alert_rule_id`. A date column given a rule in the app is missing from the `dl:rule` pick-list, so it can't be used as a filter and sorts after the others when grouped. A column whose rule was removed through the column menu keeps a stale `alert_rule_name` and still shows up.
  - Fix: check `alert_rule_id` (as `starter-reports.ts:24` and `deadlines.ts` do), or build the options from the `loadDeadlines` results.

- **[bug]** `src/main/report-builder.ts:164,393` with `src/main/ipc/ipc-reports.ts:33-43`. A pinned report keeps running after its list goes to the trash.
  - What goes wrong: `getReport` and `runReport` don't check `grids.is_active`. The dashboard card keeps showing the trashed list's records. Its "Open" button lands on "That report is no longer available.", because `listReports` filters out inactive lists.
  - Fix: have `getReport` / `runReport` reject inactive lists so the card hides itself (SavedReportCard already returns null on that message), or unpin when a list is trashed.

## Visible

- **[visible]** `src/renderer/views/dashboard/DeadlineRow.tsx:76`. The tooltip disagrees with the row for interval rules with working days or an extension.
  - What goes wrong: for `ruleKind === 'interval'` the tooltip is `formatDeadlineTooltip(dateValue, ruleInterval, daysUntil)`, which recomputes the date without the business-day roll or the extension and pairs it with the engine's day count. Example: a row shows "Oct 9" (rolled back from Saturday), but its tooltip says "Deadline: Oct 10, 2026 · in 2 days". An extended row shows the original date.
  - Fix: build the tooltip from `deadline.due` / `deadline.detail` for every kind, using `formatResultTooltip`.

- **[visible]** `src/renderer/components/profile/DeadlinePanel.tsx:294,400`. "Next due" on the record page ignores working days, and a finished one-off shows no status.
  - What goes wrong: `nextDue = computeEffectiveDeadline(rawValue, interval)` skips the business-day roll, so the record page says "Next due Oct 10" while the grid and dashboard say Oct 9. Separately, a one-off date marked Finished (migration 046) gives `daysUntil === null`, which returns `result: null, engine: null` (line 179). The card shows the bare date, with no "Done"/"Finished" status and a grey accent. The grid chip says "✓ done".
  - Fix: take the date from `engineResult.due`, and keep `engine`/`result` when `engineResult.state === 'done'`.

- **[visible]** `src/renderer/utils/deadlineCalc.ts:82-89` (used at `SortableRow.tsx:302`). The grid tooltip and screen-reader text say "Due today" for a used-up quota on a Date column.
  - What goes wrong: the tooltip reads "Deadline: Dec 31, 2026 · Due today", while the chip next to it correctly says "All 12 used".
  - Fix: return `res.label` (or "All used · ends …") when `isUsedUp(res)`, as `deadlineChipText` does.

- **[visible]** `src/renderer/components/grid/CellDisplay.tsx:105` (sort at `DataGrid.tsx:451`). A "Days until" column ignores the rule on its source date.
  - What goes wrong: `computeDaysUntil(sourceValue)` counts to the stored date. Suppose the source is "Last visit" with "every 30 days". The stored date is the last-done date, so "Days until" shows "25 days overdue ⚠️" in red, while the same row's deadline chip says "in 5 days".
  - Fix: when the source column has a rule, use `evaluateRuleForRow(...).daysUntil`.

- **[visible]** `src/shared/reports.ts:288`. The report explanation makes up singular words.
  - What goes wrong: `src.replace(/s$/, '')` turns plurals from the starters into "Counting 1 propertie", "1 companie", "1 facilitie" and "1 beneficiarie". "1 children", "1 people" and "1 staff" stay plural. This shows on the Reports home cards (REPORT_RUN_SAVED) and under the builder (`ReportBuilder.tsx:472`).
  - Fix: pass the singular word too (`word('record', {}, gridId)` / `t('record')`) instead of stripping an "s".

- **[visible]** `src/renderer/views/ReportsView.tsx:117,127`. Saving a new report briefly says "That report is no longer available."
  - What goes wrong: `onSaved` starts `load()` and sets `?id=<new>` straight away. On the next render `reports` is still the old list, so the "no longer available" branch shows until REPORT_LIST returns. The "Pin to dashboard" path for an unsaved report hits the same flash.
  - Fix: insert the saved report into `reports` first (`setReports(rs => [...rs, r])`), then set the param.

- **[visible]** `src/renderer/components/grid/CellDisplay.tsx:231`. A Deadline column on a calendar rule shows "—" most of the time.
  - What goes wrong: before the lead time (and right after Done) the engine returns `due: null` with `upcoming`/`detail` "Next: Jan 15, 2026", but DeadlineCell renders a bare "—" with no tooltip. A quarterly-estimate column looks empty for about 10 weeks of every quarter, and Done seems to wipe it.
  - Fix: when `res.upcoming` is set, render a muted "Next {date}" with `title={res.detail}`.

## Polish

- **[polish]** `src/main/export-report.ts:23`. The report PDF doesn't wrap its title.
  - What goes wrong: `doc.text(title, …)` at 16pt bold isn't wrapped. Names can be up to 100 characters, and suggested names like "Total Estimate by Stage and per month, this quarter" run past the right margin.
  - Fix: use `splitTextToSize` on the title, as the subtitle already does, and move `tableTop` down to match.

- **[polish]** `src/renderer/components/alerts/AlertRuleEditor.tsx:371`. The rule editor's ✕ close button is icon-only, with no `aria-label` or `title`.
  - What goes wrong: screen readers announce it as "button".
  - Fix: add `aria-label="Close"`.

- **[polish]** `src/renderer/views/dashboard/GettingStartedCard.tsx:47-49`, `src/renderer/views/dashboard/DeadlineRow.tsx:153`, `src/renderer/views/dashboard/cards/TodayCard.tsx:48`, `src/renderer/components/reports/CalendarExportDialog.tsx:58`. Words that should come from the vocabulary layer are hard-coded.
  - Examples: "Open your list" (×3), "Open the record to set new dates…", "Marking them writes the note…", "Note text is never included." A workspace that calls lists "caseloads" and notes "case notes" sees the generic words.
  - Fix: use `t('list')`, `t('record')` and `t('activity')`.

- **[polish]** `src/main/report-builder.ts:97` (`columnKind` at line 40). A Deadline column in a records report shows its done date under the column's name.
  - What goes wrong: for `column_type 'deadline'` the stored value is the last-done date. A records report column "Annual review" shows "Sep 1, 2026", while the grid's "Annual review" shows "Due Mar 1, 2027 · in 141 days".
  - Fix: label these fields "<name> (last done)", or add a worked-out "<name>: due" field from `loadDeadlines`.
