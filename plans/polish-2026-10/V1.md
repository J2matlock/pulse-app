# V1 — visual sweep (2026-10-11)

How: Playwright + built `out/`, fresh workspace per starter with sample data. Starters: Foster Care Caseworker (human services, light), Tree service (trades, dark), Auto & equipment service (trades, light), Animal rescue (animals, light), Therapy & counseling (health, light), Family health (personal, dark). Each covered: main list at 1366x768, profile, dashboard, reports home + one report, calendar, tasks, contacts, settings (Appearance and Deadlines tabs), More / Filter / Views / column-header / cell context menus, a date editor near the bottom-right, then the list, More menu, profile and dashboard at 1024x700. Screenshots are in `plans/polish-2026-10/screens/` (`<starter>-<screen>.png`).

Ordered by how much they hurt. Low-confidence items are marked (verify).
