# R1 — Grid, cells and board

- **[visible]** `src/renderer/components/grid/SortableRow.tsx:475` + `src/shared/starters/*.json` (e.g. `auto-service.json:168`, `behavior-analyst.json:112`). Every one of the 273 rule-bearing date/deadline columns in the starters is 130–180px wide (most are 150). A date cell with a rule now shows four things: the padding (24px), the shape icon (18px), the date (about 80px at 15px) and the chip. At 150px that leaves the chip about 20px, so "in 23 days" or "due Mar 4 · in 52 days" shows as "i…" or just "…". The deadline words (G-09) are effectively invisible in every starter list. For interval rules like "Last oil change" the chip is "due <date> · in N days" (the stored value isn't the due date), which needs about 290px. The cell only gets a `title` when a color band matches (`alertTooltip`), so far-off, done and paused chips are cut with no tooltip. Fix: widen rule-bearing date columns in the starters (about 260–300) and when a rule is attached, give the cell a tooltip from `deadlineChipText`/`formatResultTooltip` always, and consider showing the chip in place of the date when the column is narrow.
- **[visible]** `src/renderer/components/grid/CellDisplay.tsx:229-236` (DeadlineCell). The spans have no `truncate`/`whitespace-nowrap`, so in a flex cell the text wraps onto two or three lines. Example: "3 of 20 used · Dec 31, 2026 · in 81 days" in the 140px "Sessions left"/"Lessons left" columns. The cell is now `overflow-hidden` with a fixed height, so the top and bottom lines are cut through the middle. The same happens to "Done Dec 31, 2026" and to step labels "Intake (step 1 of 4) · …". Fix: add `truncate min-w-0` to each DeadlineCell span (keep `title={res.detail}`), and widen the starter deadline columns.
- **[bug]** `src/renderer/components/grid/CellDisplay.tsx:40,218-236` + `SortableRow.tsx:473`. `DeadlineCell` reads `deadlineContext` (quota counts, meter history, steps, done flags) through `useGridStore.getState()`, but it sits inside `React.memo(CellDisplay)`, whose props don't change when the context arrives after the rows (`gridStore.ts:262`). The rows on screen at load keep the text worked out without context: "0 of 12 used", the wrong step, not "Done". Meanwhile the color and icon from SortableRow do update, so text and color disagree until the row is edited or scrolled out and back. Fix: pass `deadlineContext` into CellDisplay (or have DeadlineCell subscribe with `useGridStore(s => s.deadlineContext)`).
