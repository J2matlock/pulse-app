# Victim advocate: Keep people safe, and keep every court date and deadline

*Human services · starter `victim-advocate`*

## The problem

Protective orders run out, hearings get continued, and the compensation claim has a deadline nobody mentions until it has passed. Every call has to go through the one safe number or the trusted sister, and that detail lives in your head or on a sticky note. One slip can put someone at risk.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For victim advocates: safe ways to reach each person, protective order expiry, court dates with reminders, safety plan check-ins every 30 days and the crime victim compensation claim deadline.

### Advocacy caseload

People you support, with safe ways to reach them, protective orders and claims.

- Deadline columns: Compensation claim filed, Protective order expires, Last safety check-in, Restitution follow-up
- Deadline rule — Protective order (30 days ahead): the date the order runs out; warns 30 days ahead so there is time to ask for an extension
- Deadline rule — Every 30 days: due 30 days after the date entered, while the case is open
- Deadline rule — Compensation claim deadline: due 1 year after the incident date. Check your state's deadline and change this rule if it is different
- Deadline rule — Due date: the date entered is the deadline
- Form: Intake
- Checklist: Safety plan
- Also: Safe contact method, Safe phone, Safe times and notes, Referred by, Incident date, Status

### Court dates

Hearings for the people you support, with reminders and results.

- Deadline columns: Hearing date, Client reminded
- Deadline rule — Hearing: the hearing date, until a result is recorded
- Deadline rule — Remind 2 days before: remind the client 2 days before the hearing, until a result is recorded
- Linked to Advocacy caseload ("Client" here, "Court dates" there)
- Also: Type, Court, Going with them, Result, Notes

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Note, Safety check-in, Court accompaniment, Phone, Referral
- Letters: Court date reminder, Protective order renewal reminder
- Sample clients to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your clients never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
