# Refugee resettlement: Arrival to 180 days, every milestone tracked

*Human services · starter `refugee-resettlement`*

## The problem

From the day a family lands, the clock is running: the home visit within days, core services within 30, check-ins at 90 and 180. Each household has its own arrival date, so every family is on a different schedule. Keeping that straight in a shared spreadsheet is a full-time job on its own.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For resettlement case managers: milestones from arrival (home visit in 5 days, core services in 30 days, 90- and 180-day check-ins) and two contacts a month.

### Resettlement caseload

Families from arrival through the first six months.

- Deadline columns: Milestones, Last contact
- Deadline rule — Resettlement milestones: from arrival: home visit in 5 days, core services in 30 days, then 90- and 180-day check-ins
- Deadline rule — Every 30 days: due 30 days after the date entered
- Contact: 2 a month
- Also: Country of origin, Language, Arrival date, Household size, Address, Phone, Employment

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Cultural orientation, Appointment escort
- Sample clients to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your clients never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
