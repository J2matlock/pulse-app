# Business licenses and permits: Every license and permit renewed before it lapses

*Business · starter `business-licenses`*

## The problem

Business license, food permit, alarm permit, sales tax registration, fire certificate. Each comes from a different office, renews on its own schedule and has its own fee. The renewal notice gets lost in the mail, and you find out when the fine arrives.

## What Pulse sets up for you

Track every license, permit and registration your business holds, with warnings 90, 60, 30 and 7 days before each expires and a renewal checklist.

### Licenses & permits

Every license, permit and registration, with its expiry date.

- Deadline columns: Expires on
- Deadline rule — Expires (90-day warning): the date entered is when it expires; warns 90, 60, 30, 7 days ahead
- Checklist: Renewal steps
- Also: Issued by, Number, Location, Issued on, Renewal fee, Renewal steps, Status

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Note, Application sent, Payment made, Inspection
- Sample licenses to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your licenses never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
