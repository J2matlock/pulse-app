# Mentoring program: Every match made, checked in on and supported

*Faith and community · starter `mentoring-program`*

## The problem

Mentees wait to be matched, mentors need background checks renewed, and every match needs a check-in each month. When the program grows past a dozen pairs, the spreadsheet stops telling you who needs attention.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For mentoring programs: mentors and mentees, one-to-one matches, mentees on a board from referral to match, background checks and a match check-in every month.

### Mentors

Volunteer mentors.

- Deadline columns: Background check
- Deadline rule — Every 2 years: due 2 years after the date entered
- Also: Phone, Email, Interests, Training done, Status

### Mentees

Young people waiting for or in a match.

- Deadline columns: Last match check-in
- Deadline rule — Every 30 days: due 30 days after the date entered
- Linked to Mentors ("Matched with" here, "Mentee" there)
- Board view by Stage: Referred → Matching → Matched → Closed
- Match check-in: 1 a month
- Also: Date of birth, Guardian, Guardian phone, School, Interests, Referred on, Stage, Match date

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Match check-in
- Sample mentors to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your mentors never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
