# Family health: Every checkup, cleaning and refill for the whole family

*Personal · starter `family-health`*

## The problem

The baby's next checkup is at some number of months, the dentist wants everyone back every six months, someone's prescription runs out next week, and flu shots are due again. It all lives in your head, a fridge calendar and three patient portals that don't talk to each other.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For keeping your family's health on track: kids' checkups by age, yearly physicals, dentist every 6 months, eye exams, flu shots, refills and insurance renewals, with doctors and appointments in one place. It isn't medical advice: follow your doctor's schedule.

### Family

Everyone in the household, with checkups, cleanings and refills due.

- Deadline columns: Checkups, Last physical, Last dental cleaning, Last eye exam, Flu shot, Next refill, Insurance renews
- Deadline rule — Well-child checkups: checkups by age, counted from the date of birth: 1, 2, 4, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18 months, 2 years, 30 months, then every year
- Deadline rule — Yearly physical: due a year after the last physical (adults)
- Deadline rule — Every 6 months: due 6 months after the date entered
- Deadline rule — Every year: due on the same date a year later
- Deadline rule — Flu shot: every fall, by the end of October
- Deadline rule — Due date: the date entered is the deadline
- Deadline rule — Renewal: the date entered is when it renews or runs out
- Linked to Doctors & dentists ("Doctors" here, "Sees" there)
- Also: Who, Date of birth, Allergies, Medications

### Doctors & dentists

The doctors, dentists, eye doctors and pharmacy your family uses.

- Also: Kind, Practice, Phone, Address, Patient portal, Notes

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Note, Doctor visit, Dentist, Medication change, Sick day
- Sample family members to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your family members never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.

## Get Pulse — free
