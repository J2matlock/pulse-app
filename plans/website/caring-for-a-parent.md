# Caring for a parent: Doctor visits, medications and paperwork for the people you care for

*Personal · starter `caring-for-a-parent`*

## The problem

Mom has a cardiologist, a primary doctor and a pharmacy; Dad has physical therapy twice a week and a power of attorney to finish. Medication reviews, insurance renewals and benefit paperwork all come due, and the details live in your phone, a folder and your memory.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For family caregivers: a parent's doctor visits on the calendar, medication reviews every 3 months, yearly check-ups, insurance and benefits renewals, their care team, and copies of the power of attorney and insurance cards kept safe in Files.

### Parents

The people you look after, with their check-ups, medications and renewals.

- Deadline columns: Medication review, Wellness visit, Insurance renews, Benefits renewal
- Deadline rule — Every 3 months: due 3 months after the date entered
- Deadline rule — Every year: due on the same date a year later
- Deadline rule — Renews (60-day warning): the date entered is when it renews
- Linked to Care team ("Care team" here, "Looks after" there)
- Checklist: Hospital stay
- Also: Date of birth, Phone, Address, Allergies, Insurance, Power of attorney, Health care proxy

### Care team

Doctors, the pharmacy and everyone else who helps.

- Also: Role, Phone, Fax, Address, Patient portal

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Doctor visit, Medication change
- Sample parents to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your parents never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
