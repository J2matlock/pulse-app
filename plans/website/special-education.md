# Special education: Every timeline from referral to IEP, in school days

*Education and youth · starter `special-education`*

## The problem

Consent within 10 school days, evaluation within 60, eligibility, then the IEP within 30 days. Annual reviews and re-evaluations every three years. Holidays and breaks don't count, and every student started on a different day. Missing a timeline is a compliance finding.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For IEP case managers and coordinators: the referral timeline (consent, evaluation in 60 school days, eligibility, first IEP), annual IEP reviews and 3-year re-evaluations.

### Special education

Students from referral through annual reviews and re-evaluations.

- Deadline columns: Referral timeline, IEP date, Last evaluation
- Deadline rule — Referral timeline: from referral: consent, evaluation in 60 school days, eligibility, then the first IEP within 30 days
- Deadline rule — Every year: due on the same date a year later
- Deadline rule — Every 3 years: due 3 years after the date entered
- Also: Grade, School, Disability category, Referral date, Parent/guardian, Parent phone, Status

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Parent contact, Meeting
- Sample students to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your students never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
