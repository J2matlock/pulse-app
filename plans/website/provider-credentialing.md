# Provider credentialing: Licenses, DEA, CAQH and payer dates for every clinician

*Health · starter `provider-credentialing`*

## The problem

Each clinician has a state license, DEA registration, board certification, malpractice policy and hospital privileges, all expiring at different times. CAQH needs re-attestation every 120 days and payers re-credential every three years. One missed date means claims get denied.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For a medical office: state licenses, DEA, board certification, malpractice, hospital privileges, CAQH re-attestation every 120 days and payer re-credentialing every 3 years.

### Credentialing

Licenses, certifications and re-credentialing for each clinician.

- Deadline columns: State license expires, DEA expires, Board certification expires, CAQH re-attestation, Malpractice insurance expires, Payer re-credentialing, Hospital privileges expire
- Deadline rule — Expires (90-day warning): the date entered is when it expires; warns 90, 60, 30, 7 days ahead
- Deadline rule — Every 120 days: due 120 days after the date entered
- Deadline rule — Every 3 years: due 3 years after the date entered
- Also: Credentials, NPI

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Note, Application sent, Approved
- Sample providers to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your providers never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
