# Home maintenance: Filters, gutters and the furnace, before they become a repair

*Personal · starter `home-maintenance`*

## The problem

The furnace filter was due sometime last winter, nobody remembers when the water heater was last flushed, and the warranty paperwork is in a drawer somewhere. Gutters need cleaning every spring and fall, the property tax bill comes twice a year, and the number for the good plumber is on a magnet that fell behind the fridge.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For homeowners: furnace filters every 90 days, yearly service for the furnace and water heater, smoke detector batteries, gutters every spring and fall, warranties, home insurance and property tax due dates, and who to call for repairs.

### Home items

The things in your home that need regular care.

- Deadline columns: Warranty ends, Air filter changed, Water filter changed, Last serviced, Batteries changed, Spring and fall upkeep
- Deadline rule — Every 90 days: due 90 days after the date entered
- Deadline rule — Every 6 months: due 6 months after the date entered
- Deadline rule — Every year: due on the same date a year later
- Deadline rule — Expires (60 days ahead): the date entered is when it runs out; warns 60 days ahead
- Deadline rule — Spring and fall: due April 30 and October 30, for items marked Spring and fall
- Linked to Service people ("Who services it" here, "Takes care of" there)
- Checklist: Spring home checklist
- Checklist: Fall home checklist
- Also: Where, Brand and model, Installed, Seasonal, Notes

### Bills and renewals

Home insurance, property tax and other house bills that come due once or twice a year.

- Deadline columns: Due
- Deadline rule — Due date: the date entered is when it is due
- Also: Company, Amount, How often, Account or policy number, Notes

### Service people

Who you call for repairs and yearly service.

- Also: Trade, Phone, Email, Last used, Notes

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Note, Call, Repair or service
- Sample items to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your items never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
