# Early intervention: IFSP on time for every child, transition never late

*Education and youth · starter `early-intervention`*

## The problem

The IFSP is due 45 days after referral, then reviews every six months and a new plan every year. The transition conference has to happen by 33 months of age. Each family is on its own clock, and a missed date is a real problem for the family and for your program.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For early intervention service coordinators: the IFSP within 45 days of referral, 6-month reviews, yearly IFSPs and transition planning at 33 months.

### Early intervention

Infants and toddlers from referral to transition at age 3.

- Deadline columns: IFSP timeline, Transition conference
- Deadline rule — IFSP timeline: from referral: the IFSP within 45 days, a 6-month review, then a yearly IFSP
- Deadline rule — Transition at 33 months: transition planning is due when the child turns 2 years 9 months
- Also: Date of birth, Parent, Parent phone, Referral date, Services, Status

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Home visit, Parent contact
- Sample children to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your children never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
