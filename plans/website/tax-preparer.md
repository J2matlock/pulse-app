# Bookkeeper & tax preparer: Every client, every return, every deadline from January to October

*Business · starter `tax-preparer`*

## The problem

Estimated taxes come due four times a year, 1099s at the end of January, returns in April and extensions in October, and half your clients still owe you a W-2. The spreadsheet holds the names, but it won't tell you who to chase this week or whose quarterly payment is coming up.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For tax preparers and bookkeepers: clients, returns on a board from waiting on documents to filed, quarterly estimated taxes, 1099s, extensions, engagement letters and monthly closes.

### Clients

People, households and small businesses you prepare taxes or keep books for.

- Deadline columns: Engagement letter signed, Estimated taxes, 1099s, Monthly close
- Deadline rule — Every year: due on the same date a year later
- Deadline rule — Estimated taxes: quarterly estimated taxes: Jan 15, Apr 15, Jun 15 and Sep 15
- Deadline rule — 1099s due: contractor 1099s are due every Jan 31
- Deadline rule — Monthly close: books closed by the 15th of every month
- Also: Phone, Email, Address, Client type, Services, Pays estimated taxes, Notes

### Returns

Each return or filing, from waiting on documents to filed.

- Deadline columns: Documents follow-up, Filing deadline, E-file accepted
- Deadline rule — Filing deadline: the date entered is the filing deadline, until the return is filed
- Deadline rule — Documents follow-up: follow up 7 days after requesting documents, while still waiting on them
- Deadline rule — E-file acceptance: check the e-file was accepted 3 days after filing
- Linked to Clients ("Client" here, "Returns" there)
- Board view by Stage: Waiting on documents → Documents in → Preparing → Review → Extended → Filed
- Checklist: Documents still missing
- Checklist: Before filing
- Also: Stage, Return type, Tax year, Documents requested on, Filed on, Fee

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Note, Call, Email, Meeting, Documents received
- Letters: Document request, Engagement letter reminder
- Sample clients to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your clients never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
