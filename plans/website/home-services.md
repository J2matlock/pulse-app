# Home services: Every customer, every property, every season

*Trades and service · starter `home-services`*

## The problem

The route lives in your head, the service plans live in a spreadsheet, and the reminders for spring tune-ups, pool openings or gutter season live nowhere at all. When a customer calls asking why nobody came back after the last treatment, you're digging through texts to find out.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For lawn care, pest control, heating and cooling, pool service, and chimney, gutter and window businesses: customers, their properties, visits on a board, seasonal and repeating service dates, and weekly routes on the calendar.

What kind of service do you run? Pick one:

- **Lawn care & landscaping** — Mowing routes, fertilizer rounds, sprinkler start-up and winterizing, and mower service by engine hours.
- **Pest control** — Quarterly service plans, a follow-up visit 14 days after each treatment, termite renewals and applicator licenses.
- **Heating & cooling (HVAC)** — Maintenance agreements with spring AC and fall furnace tune-ups, filters every 90 days, equipment warranties and tech licenses.
- **Pool service** — Weekly route visits, opening and closing each season, filter cleaning and water readings.
- **Chimney, gutters & windows** — Yearly chimney sweeps, gutter cleaning each spring and fall, window washing and dryer vents.

The first layout, Lawn care & landscaping, is shown below.

### Customers

The people and businesses you work for.

- Also: Phone, Email, Preferred contact, Billing address, Notes

### Properties

Lawns and yards you look after, with their programs and seasonal dates.

- Deadline columns: Last fertilizer round, Irrigation started, Irrigation winterized
- Deadline rule — Fertilizer rounds: four rounds a year: April, June, August and October 15
- Deadline rule — Irrigation start-up: turn the sprinklers on by the end of April
- Deadline rule — Winterize: blow out the lines by the end of October
- Linked to Customers ("Customer" here, "Properties" there)
- Also: Address, Gate and pet notes, Service plan, Lawn size (sq ft)

### Visits

Each visit from the request to done.

- Deadline columns: Scheduled for
- Deadline rule — Visit date: the date entered is the day of the visit, until it is done
- Linked to Properties ("Property" here, "Visits" there)
- Board view by Stage: Requested → Scheduled → Done → Cancelled
- Form: Service request
- Checklist: Spring start-up
- Also: Stage, Service, Completed on, Price, Notes

### Equipment

Mowers, trimmers and trucks, with service due by engine hours.

- Deadline columns: Last service, Registration expires
- Deadline rule — Service: every 50 engine hours or once a year, whichever comes first
- Deadline rule — Expires: the date entered is when it runs out
- Also: Type, Hour meter, Hours at last service, Notes

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Note, Call, Text, On site
- Letters: Seasonal service reminder

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your customers never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
