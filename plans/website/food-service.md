# Food service compliance: Pass every inspection: staff cards and equipment, on schedule

*Business · starter `food-service`*

## The problem

Food handler cards expire. The hood needs cleaning every six months, thermometers need calibrating every month, and the pest service comes monthly. The health inspector can show up any day, and the clipboard by the walk-in doesn't warn you before something is due.

## What Pulse sets up for you

Food handler cards and manager certifications for your staff, plus hood cleaning, calibration, fire suppression, pest service and health inspections for your equipment.

### Kitchen staff

Kitchen and front-of-house staff with their food safety cards.

- Deadline columns: Food handler card expires, Manager certification expires, Allergen training
- Deadline rule — Expires (60-day warning): the date entered is when it expires; warns 60, 30, 14 days ahead
- Deadline rule — Every year: due on the same date a year later
- Also: Role, Phone, Hire date

### Equipment & services

Equipment and the regular services it needs.

- Deadline columns: Last hood cleaning, Last thermometer calibration, Last fire suppression inspection, Last pest service, Last health inspection
- Deadline rule — Every 6 months: due 6 months after the date entered
- Deadline rule — Every 30 days: due 30 days after the date entered
- Deadline rule — Every year: due on the same date a year later
- Also: Location, Vendor

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Note, Inspection, Service visit
- Sample staff to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your staff never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
