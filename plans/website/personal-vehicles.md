# Personal vehicles: Oil changes, rotations and renewals for every car in the house

*Personal · starter `personal-vehicles`*

## The problem

The sticker on the windshield faded months ago. Was the last oil change at 41,000 or 44,000? Registration and insurance renew on different dates for each car, and the reminders go to an old email address.

## What Pulse sets up for you

Your household's cars: oil changes every 5,000 miles or 6 months, tire rotations, registration and insurance renewals.

### My vehicles

Your cars with oil changes, tire rotations, registration and insurance.

- Deadline columns: Last oil change, Last rotation, Registration expires, Insurance renews
- Deadline rule — Oil change: every 5,000 miles or 6 months, whichever comes first
- Deadline rule — Tire rotation: every 7,500 miles
- Deadline rule — Due date: the date entered is the deadline
- Also: Odometer, Odometer at last oil change, Odometer at last rotation, Notes

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Service
- Sample vehicles to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your vehicles never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
