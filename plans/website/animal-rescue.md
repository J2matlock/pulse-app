# Animal rescue: Every animal from intake to adoption, and every foster home

*Animals · starter `animal-rescue`*

## The problem

Animals come in, see the vet, go to fosters and, with luck, get adopted. Rabies vaccines and vet visits come due, foster homes need checking, and the volunteer who knows where every animal is just went on vacation.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For rescues and shelters: animals on a board from intake to adoption, which foster home each is with, vaccines and vet visits, and foster home checks.

### Animals

Animals from intake to adoption.

- Deadline columns: Rabies vaccine, Next vet visit
- Deadline rule — Every year: due on the same date a year later
- Deadline rule — Due date: the date entered is the deadline
- Linked to Fosters ("Placed with" here, "Animals in care" there)
- Board view by Stage: Intake → Medical → Ready → Adoption pending → Adopted
- Also: Species, Breed, Sex, Intake date, Stage, Spayed/neutered, Microchip, Adopted on

### Fosters

Foster homes and what they can take.

- Deadline columns: Home check
- Deadline rule — Every 2 years: due 2 years after the date entered
- Also: Phone, Email, Address, Can take, Status

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Vet visit, Adoption application
- Sample animals to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your animals never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
