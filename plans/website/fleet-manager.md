# Fleet manager: Service every vehicle on time, by miles or by months

*Trades and service · starter `fleet-manager`*

## The problem

Each vehicle needs service every 5,000 miles or six months, whichever comes first, plus registration and inspection every year. Odometer readings come in by text and on paper, and nobody can tell you which truck is due next week.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For small fleets: service every 5,000 miles or 6 months (Pulse estimates the date from each vehicle's mileage), registrations and annual inspections.

### Fleet

Vehicles with mileage-based service, registration and inspections.

- Deadline columns: Last service, Registration expires, Annual inspection
- Deadline rule — Service: every 5,000 miles or 6 months, whichever comes first
- Deadline rule — Due date: the date entered is the deadline
- Deadline rule — Every year: due on the same date a year later
- Also: Year, Make and model, Plate, Odometer, Odometer at last service, Assigned driver, Status

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Service, Fuel, Repair
- Sample vehicles to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your vehicles never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
