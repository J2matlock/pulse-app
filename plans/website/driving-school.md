# Driving school: From learner's permit to road test, without the paper log

*Education and youth · starter `driving-school`*

## The problem

Every student is on a different clock: a permit that has to be six months old before the road test, 50 practice hours with 10 at night, a lesson package running down, and parents who want to know how it's going. Meanwhile your instructors' licenses, background checks and car insurance all renew on dates nobody wrote down.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For driving schools and instructors: students from learner's permit to road test, practice and night hours, lessons left on a package, monthly parent updates, and instructor licenses, background checks and training cars. Hour and permit rules vary by state, so check yours.

### Students

Learners from enrollment to their road test.

- Deadline columns: Permit expires, Road test booked, Road test date, Lessons left
- Deadline rule — Road test eligible: book the road test once the permit is 6 months old
- Deadline rule — Test day: the date entered is the day of the road test, until the student passes
- Deadline rule — Expires: the date entered is when it runs out
- Deadline rule — Lessons left: counts lessons between the enrollment date and the permit expiry
- Linked to Instructors ("Instructor" here, "Students" there)
- Board view by Status: Enrolled → Learning → Ready for road test → Passed → Withdrawn
- Parent update: 1 a month
- Form: Enrollment
- Checklist: Road test day
- Also: Status, Parent / guardian, Parent phone, Permit issued, Passed on, Practice hours, Night hours, Enrolled on, Lessons paid for

### Instructors

Instructors and their training cars, with licenses and checks due.

- Deadline columns: Instructor license expires, Background check, Car insurance renews, Car safety inspection
- Deadline rule — Expires (60 days ahead): the date entered is when it runs out; warns 60 days ahead
- Deadline rule — Every 2 years: due on the same date two years later
- Deadline rule — Every year: due on the same date a year later
- Also: Phone, Email, Training car

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Note, Lesson, Road test, Parent update
- Letters: Road test reminder
- Sample students to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your students never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
