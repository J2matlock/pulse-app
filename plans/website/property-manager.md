# Property manager: Units, tenants and work orders, all connected

*Property and safety · starter `property-manager`*

## The problem

Leases end on different dates across every unit, work orders come in by phone and text, and inspections are due on their own schedule. When a tenant calls, you want their unit, their lease and their open work orders in one place, not in three spreadsheets.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For landlords and small property managers: units with inspections, tenants with lease ends, and work orders on a board from new to done.

### Units

Rental units with inspections and status.

- Deadline columns: Last inspection, Smoke detectors checked
- Deadline rule — Every year: due on the same date a year later
- Deadline rule — Every 6 months: due 6 months after the date entered
- Also: Building, Bedrooms, Rent, Status

### Tenants

Tenants and their leases.

- Deadline columns: Lease end
- Deadline rule — Expires (90-day warning): the date entered is when it expires; warns 90, 60, 30 days ahead
- Linked to Units ("Lives in" here, "Tenants" there)
- Also: Phone, Email, Lease start, Monthly rent, Deposit, Emergency contact

### Work orders

Repairs and maintenance requests.

- Deadline columns: Scheduled for
- Deadline rule — Due date: the date entered is the deadline
- Linked to Units ("Unit" here, "Work orders" there)
- Board view by Stage: New → Scheduled → In progress → Done
- Also: Stage, Priority, Reported on, Vendor, Cost, Completed on

### Also included

- Letters: Lease renewal notice
- Sample units to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your units never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
