# Truck driver qualification files: Driver qualification files that are always audit-ready

*Trades and service · starter `driver-files`*

## The problem

CDLs and medical cards expire. You need the yearly MVR and annual review for every driver, plus road tests and drug testing on file. A DOT audit means pulling every file and hoping nothing has lapsed since the last time you checked.

## What Pulse sets up for you

Driver qualification files for a small fleet: CDL and medical card expiry, yearly MVR and driving record reviews, road tests and drug testing enrollment.

### Driver files

Each driver's qualification file: license, medical card and yearly reviews.

- Deadline columns: CDL expires, Medical card expires, MVR review, Annual review
- Deadline rule — Expires (60-day warning): the date entered is when it expires; warns 60, 30, 14 days ahead
- Deadline rule — Every year: due on the same date a year later
- Also: CDL class, CDL number, Road test, Drug testing enrolled, Hire date, Status

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Note, Document received
- Letters: Medical card reminder
- Sample drivers to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your drivers never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
