# Peer recovery specialist: Celebrate every milestone, never miss a check-in

*Human services · starter `peer-recovery`*

## The problem

30 days, 60, 90, six months, a year: each milestone matters to the person you support, and you want to be there for it. Add recovery plan reviews and weekly contacts, and the dates get away from you. Your notes are scattered between a notebook and your phone.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For peer recovery and recovery coaching: a weekly contact requirement, recovery plan reviews every 90 days and milestones at 30, 60 and 90 days, 6 months and every year.

### Peer support caseload

People in recovery you meet with each week.

- Deadline columns: Milestones, Recovery plan review, Last contact
- Deadline rule — Recovery milestones: milestones counted from the recovery start date
- Deadline rule — Every 90 days: due 90 days after the date entered
- Deadline rule — Every 30 days: due 30 days after the date entered
- Weekly contact: 1 a week
- Also: Phone, Recovery start date, Supports, Emergency contact, Emergency phone, Status

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Group, Crisis contact
- Sample clients to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your clients never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
