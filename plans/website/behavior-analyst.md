# Behavior analyst (ABA): Authorized sessions, plan reviews and RBT supervision, tracked

*Health · starter `behavior-analyst`*

## The problem

Each learner has authorized sessions that run out, a treatment plan to review every six months and a guardian to keep in the loop. Your technicians need their RBT certification current and supervision every month. You track it today in three spreadsheets and your head.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For BCBAs and ABA practices: learners with authorized sessions counted as you log them, treatment plan reviews every 6 months, and technicians with RBT certification and monthly supervision.

### Learners

Learners with authorizations and treatment plan reviews.

- Deadline columns: Sessions left, Treatment plan review
- Deadline rule — Sessions left: counts sessions between the authorization start and end dates
- Deadline rule — Every 6 months: due 6 months after the date entered
- Linked to Technicians ("Technician" here, "Learners" there)
- Also: Date of birth, Guardian, Guardian phone, Funding, Sessions authorized, Authorization start, Authorization end, Status

### Technicians

Registered behavior technicians and their supervision.

- Deadline columns: RBT certification expires, Last supervision, CPR expires
- Deadline rule — Expires (60-day warning): the date entered is when it expires; warns 60, 30, 14 days ahead
- Deadline rule — Every 30 days: due 30 days after the date entered
- Also: Phone, Email, Status

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Session, Parent training
- Sample learners to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your learners never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
