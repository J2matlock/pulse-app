# Grant manager: Every report and closeout, for every funder

*Business · starter `grant-manager`*

## The problem

Quarterly reports for one funder, annual reports for another, closeout 90 days after the grant ends. Each grant has its own dates, its own contact and its own rules. One missed report can cost you next year's funding.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For nonprofit grant managers: quarterly reports, closeout reports 90 days after a grant ends, funders and program officers.

### Grants

Grants with quarterly reports and closeout deadlines.

- Deadline columns: Quarterly report, Closeout submitted
- Deadline rule — Quarterly report: progress reports are due 30 days after each quarter ends
- Deadline rule — Closeout due: the final report is due 90 days after the grant ends
- Also: Funder, Amount, Start date, End date, Program officer, Officer email, Status

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Report sent, Payment received
- Sample grants to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your grants never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
