# Tree service: Estimates, crews and equipment, without the clipboard

*Trades and service · starter `tree-service`*

## The problem

Estimate requests come in faster than you can call people back, and half the quotes you send never get a follow-up. Meanwhile the chipper is due for service by the hour meter, the bucket truck needs its yearly inspection, and somebody's CPR card ran out last month.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For tree care companies: customers, jobs on a board from estimate request to done, estimate follow-ups, permits, equipment service by engine hours, aerial lift inspections and crew certifications.

### Customers

Homeowners, businesses and property managers you work for.

- Deadline columns: Last tree check, Ash tree treatment
- Deadline rule — Every year: due on the same date a year later
- Deadline rule — Every 2 years: due on the same date two years later
- Also: Phone, Email, Address, Preferred contact, Property notes

### Jobs

Work from the first call to the cleanup check.

- Deadline columns: Estimate follow-up, Scheduled for, Permit expires, Follow-up call
- Deadline rule — Scheduled date: the date entered is the day of the job, until it is done
- Deadline rule — Expires: the date entered is when it runs out
- Deadline rule — Estimate follow-up: call back 7 days after sending an estimate, while it is still waiting
- Deadline rule — Follow-up call: call the customer 7 days after the job is done
- Linked to Customers ("Customer" here, "Jobs" there)
- Board view by Stage: Estimate requested → Estimate sent → Approved → Scheduled → In progress → Done → Not going ahead
- Form: Estimate request
- Checklist: Job site safety
- Also: Stage, Work type, Estimate, Estimate sent on, Completed on, Invoice total

### Equipment

Chippers, stump grinders, trucks and saws, with service and inspections due.

- Deadline columns: Last service, Aerial lift inspection, Dielectric test, Registration expires
- Deadline rule — Service: every 100 engine hours or 6 months, whichever comes first
- Deadline rule — Every year: due on the same date a year later
- Deadline rule — Expires: the date entered is when it runs out
- Also: Type, Hour meter, Hours at last service, Notes

### Crew

Climbers, ground workers and operators, with certifications and training due.

- Deadline columns: Arborist certification expires, CPR / first aid expires, CDL medical card expires, Chainsaw safety training
- Deadline rule — Expires (60 days ahead): the date entered is when it runs out; warns 60 days ahead
- Deadline rule — Every year: due on the same date a year later
- Also: Phone, Role

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Note, Call, Text, Site visit
- Letters: Estimate follow-up, Yearly tree check reminder
- Sample customers to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your customers never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
