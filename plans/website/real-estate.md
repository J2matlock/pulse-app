# Real estate transactions: Every deal from showing to closing, and every date in between

*Business · starter `real-estate`*

## The problem

Once a contract is signed, the clock starts on the inspection, the appraisal, the loan and the closing, and each deal runs on its own dates. Miss a contingency and your buyer loses their protection. Meanwhile pre-approval letters expire, listing agreements run out, and the closing anniversaries you meant to remember slip by.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For real estate agents: buyers and sellers, and each home on a board from showing to closing, with the inspection, appraisal, financing and closing dates counted from the contract date, pre-approval and listing expirations, and a note on every closing anniversary. Your contract sets the real dates.

### Buyers & sellers

The people you are helping buy or sell a home.

- Deadline columns: Pre-approval expires, Listing agreement expires
- Deadline rule — Expires: the date entered is when it runs out
- Also: Phone, Email, Buying or selling, Current address, Price range, Found you through, Notes

### Transactions

Each home from the first showing to closing.

- Deadline columns: Inspection done, Appraisal received, Loan approved, Closing date, Closing anniversary
- Deadline rule — Inspection period: inspection done within 10 days of the contract date
- Deadline rule — Appraisal: appraisal back within 21 days of the contract date
- Deadline rule — Financing contingency: loan approval within 21 days of the contract date
- Deadline rule — Closing: the date entered is the closing, while the deal is open
- Deadline rule — Closing anniversary: every year on the day it closed, 30 days ahead
- Linked to Buyers & sellers ("Client" here, "Transactions" there)
- Board view by Stage: Showing → Offer made → Under contract → Clear to close → Closed → Fell through
- Checklist: Under contract
- Also: Stage, Side, Price, Contract date, Closed on, Commission

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Note, Call, Text, Showing
- Letters: Under contract: key dates, Closing anniversary note
- Sample clients to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your clients never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
