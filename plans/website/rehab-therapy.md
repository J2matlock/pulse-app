# Speech, OT and PT practice: Plans of care, progress notes and authorizations, counted for you

*Health · starter `rehab-therapy`*

## The problem

Every patient is on three clocks at once: a plan of care to recertify every 90 days, a progress note due by the 10th visit, and an authorization that runs out by date or by visits, whichever comes first. Then there is the signed order you are still chasing from the doctor's office. A spreadsheet holds the dates but can't count visits.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For speech, occupational and physical therapists: plan of care recertification every 90 days, a progress note every 10 visits, authorized visits counted as you write notes, signed doctor orders within 30 days, twice-weekly visits on the calendar and an evaluation form.

### Patients

Everyone you treat, with their orders, plan of care and authorized visits.

- Deadline columns: Order signed on, Plan of care start, Visits left, Progress note due
- Deadline rule — Physician order: signed order due 30 days after the evaluation
- Deadline rule — Plan of care recert: recertify the plan of care every 90 days
- Deadline rule — Visits left: counts visits between the authorization start and end dates
- Deadline rule — Progress note every 10 visits: counts treatment visits since the last progress note; one is due by the 10th
- Linked to Referring doctors ("Referred by" here, "Patients" there)
- Board view by Stage: Evaluation → Active → On hold → Discharged
- Form: Evaluation
- Checklist: Discharge
- Also: Date of birth, Phone, Discipline, Stage, Evaluation date, Visits authorized, Authorization start, Authorization end, Last progress note

### Referring doctors

Doctors who send patients and sign plans of care.

- Also: Practice, Phone, Fax, Email, Notes

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Evaluation, Treatment visit, Progress note, Phone, No-show
- Letters: Physician signature request, Authorization request
- Sample patients to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your patients never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
