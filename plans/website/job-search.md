# Job search: Every application, follow-up and interview in one place

*Personal · starter `job-search`*

## The problem

You have sent out a dozen applications and can't remember which ones you followed up on. One posting closes Friday, an interview is Tuesday, and the thank-you note you meant to send is still in your head. A spreadsheet holds the list, but it won't tell you who to call back today.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For anyone looking for work: applications on a board from interested to an answer, follow-ups a week after applying, thank-you notes after interviews, offer deadlines, and the people who can help.

### Applications

Every job you are looking at, from interested to an answer.

- Deadline columns: Apply by, Follow-up sent, Thank-you sent, Respond to offer by
- Deadline rule — Apply by: the closing date for applications, until you apply
- Deadline rule — Follow-up: check in 7 days after applying, while you are still waiting to hear
- Deadline rule — Thank-you note: send a thank-you the day after an interview
- Deadline rule — Respond by: the date they need your answer on an offer
- Linked to Companies ("Company" here, "Applications" there)
- Board view by Stage: Interested → Applied → Interviewing → Offer → Accepted → Not this time
- Checklist: Before the interview
- Also: Stage, Found on, Pay, Posting link, Applied on, Last interview, Closed on

### Companies

Places you have applied or want to work.

- Also: Website, Contact person, Contact email, Contact phone, Address, Notes

### Contacts

References and people in your network.

- Deadline columns: Last in touch
- Deadline rule — Every 3 months: due 3 months after the date entered
- Also: Phone, Email, Works at, How you know them, Reference, Notes

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Note, Call, Email, Interview
- Letters: Follow-up on my application, Thank-you after an interview
- Sample applications to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your applications never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
