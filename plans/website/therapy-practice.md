# Therapy & counseling: Sessions, authorizations and plan reviews without the paperwork pile

*Health · starter `therapy-practice`*

## The problem

Weekly sessions, authorizations that run out, treatment plans to review every 90 days, consent forms to renew and screening scores to compare over time. Your EHR holds the records, but it doesn't tell you on Monday morning who is about to run out of sessions.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For therapists and counselors: weekly sessions on the calendar, authorized sessions counted as you write session notes, treatment plan reviews every 90 days, PHQ-9 and GAD-7 scores over time and an intake form.

### Clients

Everyone you see, with their authorization, plan review and screening scores.

- Deadline columns: Sessions left, Treatment plan review, Consent signed
- Deadline rule — Sessions left: counts sessions between the authorization start and end dates
- Deadline rule — Every 90 days: due 90 days after the date entered
- Deadline rule — Every year: due on the same date a year later
- Form: Intake
- Also: Date of birth, Phone, Insurance, Main concern, Sessions authorized, Authorization start, Authorization end, PHQ-9, GAD-7, Status

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Session, Phone, No-show, Cancellation
- Letters: Authorization renewal request
- Sample clients to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your clients never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.

## Get Pulse — free

[Download Pulse](https://github.com/J2matlock/pulse-releases/releases/latest) for Windows or Linux and pick "Therapy & counseling" when it asks how you want to start. Pulse is free and open source: no trial, no license key, no limits.
