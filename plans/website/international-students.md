# International student advisor: Every document date and OPT window, for every student

*Education and youth · starter `international-students`*

## The problem

I-20s, passports and visas expire, and OPT can only be applied for in a narrow window around the end of the program. Each student is on a different schedule, and the dates live in a database you can only search one student at a time.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For international student offices: program end dates, the OPT window (90 days before to 60 days after the program ends) and I-20, passport and visa expiries.

### International students

Students with program end dates, OPT windows and document expiries.

- Deadline columns: OPT applied, I-20 end date, Passport expires, Visa expires
- Deadline rule — OPT application window: OPT can be applied for from 90 days before to 60 days after the program ends
- Deadline rule — Expires (90-day warning): the date entered is when it expires; warns 90, 60, 30 days ahead
- Also: Country, Program, Program end date, Email, Status

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Advising
- Sample students to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your students never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.

## Get Pulse — free

[Download Pulse](https://github.com/J2matlock/pulse-releases/releases/latest) for Windows or Linux and pick "International student advisor" when it asks how you want to start. Pulse is free and open source: no trial, no license key, no limits.
