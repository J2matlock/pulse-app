# Childcare director: Immunizations, physicals and staff checks, always inspection-ready

*Education and youth · starter `childcare-center`*

## The problem

Every child needs current immunizations, a yearly physical and an updated emergency card. Every staff member needs CPR, a background check and a TB test. Licensing can visit any day, and pulling every file to check dates takes hours you don't have.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For childcare centers: children with immunization records, physicals and emergency cards, and staff with CPR, background checks, TB tests and training hours.

### Children

Enrolled children with health forms and emergency cards.

- Deadline columns: Immunizations due, Physical exam, Emergency card updated
- Deadline rule — Due date: the date entered is the deadline
- Deadline rule — Every year: due on the same date a year later
- Also: Date of birth, Classroom, Parent, Parent phone, Allergies, Start date

### Staff

Staff with certifications and checks.

- Deadline columns: CPR/First aid expires, Background check, TB test
- Deadline rule — Expires (60-day warning): the date entered is when it expires; warns 60, 30, 14 days ahead
- Deadline rule — Every 5 years: due 5 years after the date entered
- Deadline rule — Every year: due on the same date a year later
- Also: Role, Phone, Hire date, Training hours this year

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Parent contact, Incident report
- Letters: Health form reminder
- Sample children to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your children never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
