# School nurse: Every student's health plan, screening and authorization in one place

*Education and youth · starter `school-nurse`*

## The problem

Hundreds of students, and you need to know at a glance who has allergies, who needs medication at lunch and whose authorization form has expired. Vision and hearing screenings, health plan reviews and parent calls all pile up during the busiest weeks of the year.

## What Pulse sets up for you

Students with health needs: allergies, conditions, medication authorizations, health plan reviews and yearly vision and hearing screenings.

### Health roster

Students with health needs, screenings and medication authorizations.

- Deadline columns: Medication authorization expires, Health plan review, Vision screening, Hearing screening
- Deadline rule — Expires (30-day warning): the date entered is when it expires; warns 30, 14, 7 days ahead
- Deadline rule — Every year: due on the same date a year later
- Also: Grade, Date of birth, Guardian, Guardian phone, Allergies, Conditions, Immunizations complete

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Office visit, Medication given, Parent call, Note
- Letters: Medication form reminder
- Sample students to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your students never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
