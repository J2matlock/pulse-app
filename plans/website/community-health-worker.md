# Community health worker: Your contacts, screenings and referrals in one place

*Human services · starter `community-health-worker`*

## The problem

You talk with people by phone, text and at the clinic door, and every one of those contacts counts toward your monthly numbers. Screenings come due, referrals need follow-up, and the report your program asks for at the end of the month means going back through all of it by hand.

## What Pulse sets up for you

Outreach and follow-up for community health workers: two contacts a month, screenings due, health concerns and referrals.

### Community health caseload

People you support with outreach, screenings and referrals.

- Deadline columns: Last contact, Screening due
- Deadline rule — Every 30 days: due 30 days after the date entered
- Deadline rule — Due date: the date entered is the deadline
- Contacts: 2 a month
- Also: Phone, Preferred language, Address, Health concerns, Primary care clinic, Insurance, Status

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Text, Referral made
- Sample clients to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your clients never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
