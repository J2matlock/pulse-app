# HR generalist: Probation reviews, benefits enrollment and anniversaries on time

*Business · starter `hr-generalist`*

## The problem

Probation ends 90 days after hire, benefits eligibility starts after 60, and everyone gets a review on their work anniversary. With new people starting every few weeks, the dates stack up in a spreadsheet nobody checks until a manager asks.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For small-business HR: probation reviews 90 days after hire, benefits eligibility at 60 days and a yearly review on each hire anniversary.

### Employees

Employees with probation, benefits and anniversary reviews.

- Deadline columns: Probation review done, Benefits enrolled, Yearly review
- Deadline rule — Probation ends: the probation review is due 90 days after the hire date
- Deadline rule — Benefits eligibility: benefits enrollment opens 60 days after the hire date
- Deadline rule — Review on hire anniversary: the yearly review falls on the hire anniversary
- Also: Job title, Department, Manager, Hire date, I-9 complete, Status

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Meeting, Document received
- Sample employees to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your employees never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
