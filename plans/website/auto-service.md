# Auto & equipment service: Customers, their vehicles and every job, from estimate to pickup

*Trades and service · starter `auto-service`*

## The problem

You know your regulars, but not when each car is due for its next oil change or inspection. Jobs move from estimate to waiting on parts to ready, and the whiteboard doesn't tell you which customer to call back three days after pickup.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For repair shops and service businesses: customers, their vehicles or equipment, and jobs on a board from estimate to pickup, with oil-change forecasts from mileage and a follow-up call 3 days after pickup.

### Customers

People and businesses you do work for.

- Also: Phone, Email, Address, Preferred contact, Notes

### Vehicles

Vehicles and equipment, with their owners and service due.

- Deadline columns: Last oil change, State inspection
- Deadline rule — Oil change: every 5,000 miles or 6 months, whichever comes first
- Deadline rule — Every year: due on the same date a year later
- Linked to Customers ("Owner" here, "Vehicles" there)
- Also: Year, Make, Model, Plate, VIN, Odometer, Odometer at last oil change

### Jobs

Work orders from estimate to pickup.

- Deadline columns: Promised, Follow-up called
- Deadline rule — Due date: the date entered is the deadline
- Deadline rule — Follow-up call: call the customer 3 days after pickup
- Linked to Vehicles ("Vehicle" here, "Jobs" there)
- Board view by Stage: Estimate → Approved → Waiting on parts → In progress → Ready → Picked up
- Also: Stage, Concern, Estimate, Picked up on, Labor hours, Invoice total

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Note, Call, Text
- Letters: Oil change reminder
- Sample customers to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your customers never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
