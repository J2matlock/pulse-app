# Sales & follow-ups: Every lead followed up, nothing falling through the cracks

*Business · starter `sales-follow-ups`*

## The problem

Leads come in from everywhere, and each one needs a call back at the right time. You need to see who is new, who has a proposal and who has gone quiet. A full CRM is more than you need, and the spreadsheet doesn't remind you of anything.

## What Pulse sets up for you

A simple sales pipeline: leads on a board from new to won or lost, what each is worth, and when to follow up next.

### Leads

People you're talking to about work, and when to follow up.

- Deadline columns: Next follow-up
- Deadline rule — Due date: the date entered is the deadline
- Board view by Stage: New → Contacted → Proposal → Won → Lost
- Also: Company, Phone, Email, Source, Stage, Value, Closed on

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Call
- Sample leads to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your leads never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
