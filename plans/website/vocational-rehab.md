# Vocational rehabilitation: From eligibility to a job that lasts

*Human services · starter `vocational-rehab`*

## The problem

The plan is due 90 days after eligibility. After someone starts a job, you follow up 90 days later. In between are meetings, referrals and employer calls. When each person is at a different step, it's easy to lose track of whose deadline is coming up.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For VR counselors and employment specialists: the employment plan 90 days after eligibility, job starts and the 90-day follow-up before closing.

### VR caseload

People from application through 90 days of work.

- Deadline columns: Plan signed, Follow-up done, Last contact
- Deadline rule — Plan due: the employment plan is due 90 days after eligibility
- Deadline rule — Placement follow-up: close successfully after 90 days in the job
- Deadline rule — Every 30 days: due 30 days after the date entered
- Also: Phone, Application date, Eligibility date, Job goal, Job start date, Employer, Status

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Job development
- Sample clients to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your clients never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
