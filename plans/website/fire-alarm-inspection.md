# Fire & alarm inspection: Every device at every site inspected on time

*Property and safety · starter `fire-alarm-inspection`*

## The problem

Each customer has sites, and each site has extinguishers, detectors, panels and sprinklers, each with its own inspection and test dates. Hydrostatic tests every five years, inspections every year, certificates to hand over after each visit. A spreadsheet per customer doesn't scale.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For fire protection and alarm companies: customers, their sites and every device at each site, with yearly inspections and 5-year hydrostatic tests on extinguishers, and inspection certificates kept with each site.

### Customers

Businesses and building owners you inspect for.

- Also: Contact person, Phone, Email, Billing address, Contract

### Sites

Buildings and locations.

- Deadline columns: Last inspection
- Deadline rule — Every year: due on the same date a year later
- Linked to Customers ("Customer" here, "Sites" there)
- Also: Address, Monitoring account, Access notes, Certificates

### Devices

Extinguishers, detectors, panels and systems at each site.

- Deadline columns: Last inspection, Hydrostatic test
- Deadline rule — Every year: due on the same date a year later
- Deadline rule — Every 5 years: due 5 years after the date entered
- Linked to Sites ("Site" here, "Devices" there)
- Also: Type, Location, Serial number, Installed, Status

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Note, Inspection, Repair
- Letters: Inspection due notice
- Sample customers to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your customers never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
