# CASA / guardian ad litem: Court reports in on time, every visit logged

*Legal and government · starter `casa-advocate`*

## The problem

Your report is due ten business days before each hearing, and you visit the child every month. The court sets new hearing dates as cases move, and each one moves your report deadline too. Your visit notes are spread across a notebook and your phone.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For court-appointed advocates: hearings, court reports due 10 business days before each hearing, and a visit every month.

### Children

Children you advocate for, with hearings, court reports and monthly visits.

- Deadline columns: Next hearing, Court report filed, Last visit
- Deadline rule — Due date: the date entered is the deadline
- Deadline rule — Court report due: the court report is due 10 business days before the hearing
- Deadline rule — Every 30 days: due 30 days after the date entered
- Visit: 1 a month
- Also: Case number, Court, Placement, Caregiver, Caregiver phone, Status

### Also included

- Sample children to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your children never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
