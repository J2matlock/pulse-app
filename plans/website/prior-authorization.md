# Prior authorization tracker: Know how many visits are left before they run out

*Health · starter `prior-authorization`*

## The problem

Each patient's authorization allows a set number of visits between two dates. When the visits run out or the dates pass, the claims get denied. Today you count visits by hand from the schedule, and renewal requests go in late.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For therapy and clinic offices: how many authorized visits each patient has left and when each authorization ends, so renewals go in on time.

### Authorizations

Patients with insurance authorizations and how many visits are left.

- Deadline columns: Visits left
- Deadline rule — Visits left: counts visits between the authorization start and end dates
- Also: Date of birth, Insurance, Authorization number, Visits authorized, Authorization start, Authorization end, Service, Status

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Visit, No-show
- Sample patients to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your patients never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
