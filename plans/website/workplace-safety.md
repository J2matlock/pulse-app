# Workplace safety training: Safety training that stays current for every employee

*Property and safety · starter `workplace-safety`*

## The problem

Forklift certification every three years, first aid every two, hazard communication, fall protection and fit tests every year, each for every person on the floor. Before an inspection you dig through binders to prove who is current. Some years you find out someone isn't.

## What Pulse sets up for you

OSHA-style training records: forklift, first aid, hazard communication, fall protection, respirator fit tests and hearing tests, with what each employee is due for.

### Safety roster

Employees and the safety training each one is due for.

- Deadline columns: Forklift certified, First aid/CPR expires, Hazard communication, Fall protection, Respirator fit test, Hearing test
- Deadline rule — Every 3 years: due 3 years after the date entered
- Deadline rule — Expires (60-day warning): the date entered is when it expires; warns 60, 30, 14 days ahead
- Deadline rule — Every year: due on the same date a year later
- Also: Department, Hire date, Status

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Note, Training
- Sample employees to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your employees never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
