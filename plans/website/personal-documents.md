# Personal documents: Passports, licenses and cards renewed before you need them

*Personal · starter `personal-documents`*

## The problem

You find out your passport expired when you go to book a trip. Driver's licenses, car registration, the kids' passports, insurance cards: everyone in the house has a few, and they all expire at different times.

## What Pulse sets up for you

Passports, licenses, insurance policies and warranties for your household, with warnings 90, 30 and 7 days before anything expires.

### My documents

Passports, licenses, insurance and warranties, with a warning before each expires.

- Deadline columns: Expires
- Deadline rule — Expires (90-day warning): the date entered is when it expires; warns 90, 30, 7 days ahead
- Also: Belongs to, Number, Where it's kept, Notes

### Also included

- Sample items to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your items never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
