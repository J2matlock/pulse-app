# Horse barn & small herd: Every horse, every farrier visit, every shot, on time

*Animals · starter `horse-barn`*

## The problem

The farrier comes every seven weeks, but not for every horse on the same week. Coggins papers run out, spring shots sneak up, and the breeding dates live on a calendar in the tack room. When an owner asks when their horse was last trimmed or wormed, you're flipping through a notebook.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For barn managers and horse owners: farrier every 7 weeks, deworming, yearly Coggins, spring and fall shots, teeth, and breeding dates through to foaling, with each horse linked to its owner.

### Horses

Every horse in the barn, with hoof care, health and breeding dates.

- Deadline columns: Last farrier visit, Last deworming, Coggins test, Last vaccines, Teeth floated, Pregnancy check, Foaled on
- Deadline rule — Every 7 weeks: due 49 days after the date entered
- Deadline rule — Every 12 weeks: due 84 days after the date entered
- Deadline rule — Every 6 months: due 6 months after the date entered
- Deadline rule — Every year: due on the same date a year later
- Deadline rule — Pregnancy check: ultrasound 15 days after breeding, while the mare is marked Bred
- Deadline rule — Foaling due: about 340 days after breeding, while the mare is Bred or In foal
- Linked to Owners ("Owner" here, "Horses" there)
- Checklist: New horse arriving
- Also: Breed, Born, Stall, Breeding status, Breeding date

### Owners

Boarders and owners of the horses in your care.

- Deadline columns: Boarding agreement signed
- Deadline rule — Every year: due on the same date a year later
- Also: Phone, Email, Address, Board plan, Emergency contact, Emergency phone, Notes

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Note, Vet visit, Farrier, Feeding change, Injury
- Letters: Vaccines coming up, Coggins due
- Sample horses to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your horses never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
