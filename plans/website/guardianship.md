# Guardianship & fiduciary: Every ward, facility and court date in one place

*Legal and government · starter `guardianship`*

## The problem

You owe the court a report every year on each ward, a visit every month, and you have to keep Medicaid and other benefits renewed. Hearings get scheduled and moved. The details for each person are spread across files, facilities and court dockets.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For professional guardians and conservators: wards, where they live, their court cases and hearings, the yearly report to the court and a visit every month.

### Wards

People under guardianship or conservatorship.

- Deadline columns: Annual report, Last visit, Medicaid renewal
- Deadline rule — Annual report to the court: due each year on the appointment anniversary
- Deadline rule — Every 30 days: due 30 days after the date entered
- Deadline rule — Due date: the date entered is the deadline
- Linked to Facilities ("Lives at" here, "Residents" there)
- Visit: 1 a month
- Also: Date of birth, Appointment date, Physician, Powers, Status

### Facilities

Where your wards live.

- Also: Type, Phone, Address, Contact person, Notes

### Court cases

Court cases and hearings.

- Deadline columns: Next hearing
- Deadline rule — Due date: the date entered is the deadline
- Linked to Wards ("Ward" here, "Court cases" there)
- Also: Court, Judge, Attorney, Attorney phone, Notes

### Also included

- Sample wards to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your wards never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
