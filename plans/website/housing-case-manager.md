# Housing case manager: Keep every tenant housed and every recertification on time

*Human services · starter `housing-case-manager`*

## The problem

Leases end, incomes have to be recertified every year, housing plans need a review every six months, and you still owe each person a monthly contact. Miss one date and someone could lose their place. A spreadsheet can hold the dates, but it won't tell you which one is coming up next.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For housing and homeless services: monthly contacts, housing plan reviews every 6 months, lease end dates and yearly income recertification, with a one-contact-a-month requirement.

### Housing caseload

Participants with housing plans, leases and recertifications.

- Deadline columns: Last contact, Housing plan review, Lease end, Income recertification
- Deadline rule — Every 30 days: due 30 days after the date entered
- Deadline rule — Every 6 months: due 6 months after the date entered
- Deadline rule — Due date: the date entered is the deadline
- Deadline rule — Every year: due on the same date a year later
- Contact: 1 a month
- Also: Phone, Address, Program, Landlord, Landlord phone, Status

### Also included

- Sample clients to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your clients never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
