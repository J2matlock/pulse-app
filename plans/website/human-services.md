# Human services: Every client, every deadline, one screen

*Human services · starter `human-services`*

## The problem

Your caseload lives in a spreadsheet that only you understand, plus sticky notes, plus a calendar that never quite matches. Monthly contacts, annual assessments and plan reviews all come due on different clocks, and the only way to know who is overdue is to scroll and squint. Audit week is a scramble.

## What Pulse sets up for you

Caseloads for care managers, social workers, coordinators and similar roles: contacts, reviews and reassessments with deadline colors.

Which describes your work best? Pick one:

- **OPWDD Caseload** — Office for People With Developmental Disabilities — standard columns for OPWDD care coordinators.
- **Social Worker Caseload** — General social work caseload with standard deadline tracking.
- **IEP Coordinator** — Individualized Education Program tracking for school-based coordinators.
- **Probation Officer** — Probation / parole case management with compliance tracking.
- **Therapist Caseload** — Outpatient therapy client tracking with session and authorization management.
- **Care Manager / Service Coordinator** — Monthly contacts, care plan reviews, and annual reassessments for care management caseloads.
- **School Counselor** — Student check-ins, plan reviews, and parent contacts.
- **Foster Care Caseworker** — Monthly home visits, court dates, case plan reviews, and medical exams.
- **Home Health Coordinator** — Visits, recertifications, and plan-of-care signatures.
- **Re-entry Case Manager** — Post-release contacts, appointments, housing, and benefits.
- **Waiver Coordinator** — Monthly contacts, quarterly visits, service plan and level-of-care reviews.

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your clients never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
