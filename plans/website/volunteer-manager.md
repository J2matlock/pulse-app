# Volunteer manager: Every volunteer welcomed, checked and thanked

*Faith and community · starter `volunteer-manager`*

## The problem

People sign up at a fair and never make it to orientation. Background checks lapse for the roles that need them, and the regular who used to come every Saturday quietly stopped showing up two months ago. Hours live on a clipboard, so the thank-you for 500 hours never goes out.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For any nonprofit that runs on volunteers: orientation within 30 days of signing up, background checks for the roles that need them, yearly training, a nudge when someone hasn’t been in for 60 days, hours given and thank-yous.

### Volunteers

Everyone who gives their time, with orientation, checks and training due.

- Deadline columns: Orientation done, Background check, Last training, Checked in
- Deadline rule — Orientation: due 30 days after signing up
- Deadline rule — Background check: every 2 years, only for volunteers who need one
- Deadline rule — Every year: due on the same date a year later
- Deadline rule — Check-in: reach out 60 days after their last shift, while they are active
- Linked to Programs ("Serves in" here, "Volunteers" there)
- Form: Volunteer sign-up
- Checklist: New volunteer
- Also: Phone, Email, Status, Signed up, Needs background check, Last shift, Total hours, Emergency contact

### Programs

Where volunteers help, and how many each one needs.

- Also: When, Where, Volunteers needed, Lead, Background check needed, Notes

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Note, Shift, Training, Thank-you, Call
- Letters: Welcome letter, Thank-you letter
- Sample volunteers to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your volunteers never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
