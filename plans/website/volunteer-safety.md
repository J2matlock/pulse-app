# Youth volunteer safety: Every volunteer cleared and trained before they serve

*Faith and community · starter `volunteer-safety`*

## The problem

Before someone works with children, you need a background check, safety training and references, and you need them kept current. Volunteers come and go, the records live in an email folder, and you're never quite sure who is cleared for Sunday.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For churches, clubs and youth programs: background checks every 3 years and child safety training every year for everyone who serves with children.

### Volunteers

Everyone who serves with children and youth, with their background checks and training.

- Deadline columns: Background check, Child safety training
- Deadline rule — Every 3 years: due 3 years after the date entered
- Deadline rule — Every year: due on the same date a year later
- Also: Ministry or program, Phone, Email, References checked, Start date, Status

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Note, Training, Check-in
- Sample volunteers to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your volunteers never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
