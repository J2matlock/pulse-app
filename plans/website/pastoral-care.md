# Pastoral care: Remember every birthday, anniversary and grieving family

*Faith and community · starter `pastoral-care`*

## The problem

You want to call on the anniversary, send a card on the birthday and check on families at 1, 3, 6 and 13 months after a loss. Your members are counting on you to remember, and a paper calendar can't keep up with a whole congregation.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For pastors and care teams: birthdays and anniversaries every year, bereavement care at 1, 3, 6 and 13 months after a loss, and regular visits.

### Care list

Members to remember on birthdays, anniversaries and after a loss.

- Deadline columns: Next birthday, Next anniversary, Bereavement care, Last visit
- Deadline rule — Birthday: each year on their birthday
- Deadline rule — Wedding anniversary: each year on the anniversary
- Deadline rule — Bereavement care: reach out 1, 3, 6 and 13 months after a loss
- Deadline rule — Every 90 days: due 90 days after the date entered
- Also: Phone, Address, Birthday, Anniversary, Date of loss, Care needs

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Card sent, Prayer
- Sample members to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your members never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
