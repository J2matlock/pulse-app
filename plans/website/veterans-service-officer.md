# Veterans service officer: Intent to file and appeal windows that never close on a veteran

*Legal and government · starter `veterans-service-officer`*

## The problem

An intent to file holds the effective date for a year. Decisions come with appeal windows. Every veteran's claim is at a different step, and a missed date can cost them months of benefits.

## What Pulse sets up for you

For county and nonprofit veterans service officers: intent-to-file deadlines one year out, claims, decisions and appeal windows.

### Veterans

Veterans with intent-to-file deadlines, claims and appeal windows.

- Deadline columns: Claim filed, Appeal filed, Next appointment
- Deadline rule — Intent to file expires: the claim must be filed within a year of the intent to file
- Deadline rule — Appeal window: A decision can be appealed for a year
- Deadline rule — Due date: the date entered is the deadline
- Also: Branch, Phone, Claim type, Intent to file date, Decision date, Status

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Office visit, Evidence received
- Sample veterans to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your veterans never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
