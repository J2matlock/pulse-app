# Staff credentials: Never get caught with an expired certification

*Business · starter `staff-credentials`*

## The problem

CPR cards, TB tests, background checks, licenses: every staff member has a handful, each expiring on its own date. You find out one has lapsed when the inspector asks for it. The spreadsheet has the dates, but nobody looks at it until it's too late.

## What Pulse sets up for you

Keep every staff member's certifications, TB tests, background checks and licenses current, with warnings before anything expires.

### Staff roster

Staff with the certificates, tests and checks each one needs to keep current.

- Deadline columns: CPR/First aid expires, TB test date, Background check date, License expires
- Deadline rule — Expires (60-day warning): the date entered is when it expires; warns 60, 30, 14 days ahead
- Deadline rule — Every year: due on the same date a year later
- Deadline rule — Every 2 years: due 2 years after the date entered
- Also: Role, Hire date, Phone, Email, License number, Orientation done, Status

### Also included

- Kinds of notes: Note, Reminder sent, Document received
- Letters: Renewal reminder
- Sample staff to try it out, removed in one click when you're ready

## Your spreadsheet, but safer

- **Stays on your computer.** No cloud account and no upload. Your staff never leave your machine.
- **Locked with your password.** Everything is encrypted, and Pulse locks itself when you step away.
- **Backed up for you.** Automatic backups, plus a recovery key in case you forget your password.
- **Colors that tell you what to do next.** Green is fine, yellow is coming up, red is overdue.
- **Bring what you have.** Import your existing spreadsheet from Excel or CSV.
